## Kyurem — The Boundary Pokémon

Kyurem fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

> **Kyurem eats what happens. Every event feeds it, and the emptier the battlefield gets, the harder
> Kyurem is to hurt. Heat and pressure are the only things that put energy back.**

One number runs the fight, and it is announced every time it moves between bands.

---

### ENTROPY — 0 to 10, and it owns the guard

Every other Sword of Justice owns its guard from its phase or its meter alone. **Kyurem's guard is
owned by the meter and the phase together — and the meter is the half you control.**

```
  ENTROPY   0-2 THIN   3-4 DRAWN   5-7 DEEP   8-10 ABSOLUTE
  Phase 0      86         89          92         94
  Phase 1      88         90          93         95
  Phase 2      90         92          94         96
  Phase 3      a flat 97 — the lock is not meant to be out-damaged

  while it charges a freeze, or wears a borrowed dragon:  +2
  while it is REELING from a broken charge or resonance:  78 / 80 / 82
```

**What raises it**

```
  +1  every turn, on its own — the world runs down
  +1  your side recovered HP this turn
  +1  the Energy Drain found something to take
  +2  a Pokémon fainted, either side's
  +2  an ABSOLUTE ZERO / GLACIATE landed
```

**What lowers it**

```
  -1  you took >= 6% of Kyurem's max HP off it this turn
  -2  a Fire- or Fighting-type move landed on it this turn (once per turn)
  -2  you broke an ABSOLUTE ZERO charge
  -3  you tore a DRAGON RESONANCE loose
```

You are not fighting for damage windows here. You are fighting to keep the battlefield **populated**.
Healing your own Pokémon, losing one, and letting Kyurem strip your screens all make it tougher.
Hitting it hard, and hitting it with heat, make it softer.

Fire and Fighting are the *thematic* answer, not the type-chart answer — Ice/Dragon is only neutral
to Fire. The fight wants heat anyway, and rewards a player who brings it.

---

### FROZEN LAWS — from Entropy 5

At Entropy 5 or higher Kyurem imposes one of four laws for **3 turns**, rotating one per imposition,
so a repeat fight does not open the same way:

| Law | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **FROZEN SWITCHING** | Your side is trapped. Ghost-types and Baton Pass still leave. |
| **FROZEN MOVES** | Your last move is Disabled. |
| **FROZEN FIELD** | Weather and terrain are stripped, now and at the top of every turn it holds. |
| **FROZEN STATS** | Your stat stages are wiped, now and at the top of every turn it holds. |

A law only lifts when its three turns run out, and a landed freeze holds it three turns longer.

---

### ABSOLUTE ZERO / GLACIATE — the charge

Kyurem **tells you before you pick a move** that it is drawing the heat out of the air. The charge
takes **3 turns in Phase 0, 2 after that**, and while it gathers Kyurem's guard is 2 points harder.

**Break it on any turn of the charge**, with either:

- a **Fire- or Fighting-type move** landing on Kyurem, or
- taking **15% or more of Kyurem's max HP** off it in one turn.

A broken charge leaves Kyurem **REELING**: it loses its next action, drops −1 Defence and −1 Special
Defence, and its guard falls to 78 / 80 / 82 for a **full turn**. That is the biggest damage window
in the fight.

If it lands instead:

```
  22% / 26% / 30% of your side's max HP, escalating with each freeze
  your stat stages wiped, your screens taken, permanent SNOW put up
  Entropy +2
  from Phase 1 it also freezes the standing law three turns deeper
      and shakes off whatever status you put on Kyurem
```

The snow is not decoration: once Kyurem has Blizzard, snow makes it never miss.

### The phases

| HP | Phase | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| start | **BOUNDARY** | The Entropy engine: the drain, the laws, the charges |
| ≤ 50% | **EMPTY DRAGON** | Slot 4 becomes Blizzard, raw Sp. Atk +12%, Entropy +2. Absolute Zero now resolves as **GLACIATE**, and at Entropy 8+ **your whole side is Heal Blocked every turn** |
| ≤ 25% | **BROKEN DRAGON** | Raw Speed +10%, Entropy +1. **DRAGON RESONANCE** begins |
| ≤ 12% | **ABSOLUTE ZERO** | The lock. See below |
| the SHATTER | **SPENT** | Kyurem drops to 6% HP, everything comes off, the ball is allowed |

---

### ENERGY DRAIN — from Entropy 3

At the end of every turn Kyurem takes the **first thing it finds**, in this order: your **screens**,
then your **stat stages**, then the **weather and terrain**. Taking something feeds it another point.
If there is nothing left to take, it says so — and that is the only turn the drain costs you nothing.

---

### DRAGON RESONANCE and UNSTABLE FUSION — Phase 2

Kyurem reaches for Reshiram or Zekrom. **Which one, and how much it gets, depends on your Pokédex.**
If you have caught or even just seen one of them, it remembers that dragon and reaches with its full
strength; if you have met both, it picks one at random; if you have met neither, only a faint echo
answers and the payoff is halved.

The absorb resolves at the **end of the following turn**, and it breaks on exactly the same two
things a charge does — heat, or 15% of its max HP in one turn. So you get two turns to tear it
loose, and doing so costs Kyurem **3 Entropy** and leaves it REELING. A charge can be gathering at
the same time; one Fire or Fighting hit breaks both.

If you let it finish, Kyurem becomes **Kyurem-White or Kyurem-Black** for **4 turns**: raw Sp. Atk
+20% and Speed +10% (halved on the faint echo), Fusion Flare or Fusion Bolt in slot 4, Turboblaze or
Teravolt for an ability, and its guard 2 points harder. Then the borrowed form **tears itself apart**
— that is the instability — and it costs Kyurem 6% of its max HP on the way out. It can reach again
three turns later.

---

### The lock, at 12% — and how to end it

At 12% everything stops:

- Your side is **trapped permanently**.
- Your side is **Heal Blocked every turn**.
- The **weather and terrain are stripped every turn**.
- An **ABSOLUTE ZERO lands every second turn** for a flat 18%, and shakes off any status on Kyurem.
- The guard is a flat **97**. You are not going to out-damage this.

The meter resets and now reads as **ENERGY, 0 to 6 — and you are the one filling it:**

```
  +2  a Fire- or Fighting-type move lands on Kyurem
  +1  you take >= 5% of its max HP off it this turn
  +1  you get weather or terrain to stick through the turn
```

At **6** the ice **SHATTERS**. Kyurem falls out of its own cold at **6% HP**, every restriction comes
off, and **only then can you throw a ball.**

Without a Fire or Fighting move at all you fill ENERGY at 2 a turn instead of 3 or 4, so the lock
lasts a turn or two longer. It is still winnable — but this fight wants heat.

---

### Its moves

```
  Ice Beam        100% accurate Ice STAB, the spine of its damage
  Dragon Pulse    special Dragon STAB
  Shadow Ball     coverage, with a Sp. Def drop that compounds
  Imprison        locks you out of any of those three you also carry  -> Blizzard from Phase 1
                                                                     -> Fusion Flare / Bolt while fused
  Ability: Pressure — every move you use against it costs double PP
```

Steel-types resist both of its STABs and it has no legal answer to them. That is deliberate: every
scripted number in this fight is a percentage of **your** max HP and completely type-blind, so a
Steel wall changes which half of the fight hurts, not whether it does.

---

### What does not work

- **OHKO moves, fixed damage, Pain Split, Destiny Bond, Perish Song** — all refused.
- **Toxic** — it lands, but the damage never ramps.
- **A 4x weakness** — incoming effectiveness is capped at 2x.
- **Setup and evasion** — FROZEN STATS wipes your stages every turn it holds, a landed freeze wipes
  them outright, and the biggest numbers in the fight ignore stats entirely.
- **Stalling** — Pressure doubles what you spend, and Entropy climbs +1 for every turn you do nothing.
- **Status** — this one *does* work. In Phase 0 it sticks and works completely, which makes it the
  strongest opening play in the fight. From Phase 1 every landed freeze shakes it off, so you have to
  re-apply it around the freeze cycle.
- **Healing** — allowed, and never free: it feeds Entropy +1. From Phase 1 the VOID blocks it outright
  whenever Entropy is 8 or higher, and the lock blocks it always.
