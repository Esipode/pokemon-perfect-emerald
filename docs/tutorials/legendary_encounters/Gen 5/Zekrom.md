## Zekrom — The Deep Black Pokémon

Zekrom fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

Its ability is **Teravolt**. Your Pokémon's ability is ignored outright — Volt Absorb, Lightning
Rod, Motor Drive, Levitate, Wonder Guard and every other defensive ability simply do not apply here.

> **Zekrom answers commitment with commitment.**

Reshiram asks what is true. Zekrom asks what you *believe*. It picks an **Ideal**, says it out loud,
and commits to it. Standing your ground makes it more certain. Contradicting the specific Ideal it
declared is the only thing that makes it doubt.

---

### CONVICTION — 0 to 5

One meter, announced in bands every time it crosses one, in **both** directions.

```
 0–1  DOUBT       it is unsure of you
 2–3  CONVICTION  it is settling into its choice
 4    CERTAINTY   it is certain of its course
 5    ABSOLUTE    it believes absolutely
```

CONVICTION owns three things at once:

- **Zekrom's damage reduction.** Every band it climbs, your hits get worse.
- **Whether Bolt Strike happens at all.** The cycle only opens at **Conviction 3 or higher**.
- **How hard Bolt Strike hits.** At Conviction 5 every payload gains 4 more points, and Zekrom
  fights at a raised critical-hit rate.

The meter moves **exactly one step per turn**, in one direction or neither:

```
  −1  you contradicted the declared Ideal
  +1  otherwise, if you stood your ground:
        • you did not switch AND you used a damaging move
        • or you are holding any positive stat stage
        • or you attacked while at 33% HP or less
   0  neither
```

Note the trap in the middle line. **Setting up feeds it.** Swords Dance teams climb this meter
faster than anyone else.

---

### THE IDEALS — read the declaration, play against it

> **"ZEKROM HAS CHOSEN ITS PATH."**

Every declaration is followed by one line naming the Ideal. It holds for **four turns**, then
wavers and Zekrom picks again (it can re-pick the same one).

| It says | The Ideal | Its stance | What contradicts it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "It believes in overwhelming force." | **POWER** | +2 Attack | **End the turn at 50% HP or better, or behind a Protect** |
| "It braces to outlast you." | **ENDURANCE** | +1 Def, +1 Sp. Def, and it heals 5% every turn | **Take 8% or more of its max HP off in one turn** |
| "It believes nothing can be fast enough." | **SPEED** | +2 Speed and permanent Tailwind | **Be at or above its Speed at the end of the turn** |
| "It will not be moved from its course." | **RESOLVE** | It **traps you** — no switching, no fleeing | **Land a status on it, or a negative Atk / Sp. Atk / Speed stage** |

When you get it right:

> **"ZEKROM'S IDEALS HAVE BEEN SHAKEN!"** — preceded by a line naming what did it.

Each Ideal also changes its Bolt Strike. That is telegraphed too, on the turn the charge begins.

---

### BOLT STRIKE — from Conviction 3

While Conviction is **3 or higher**, Zekrom announces a strike ("Black lightning gathers around
Zekrom's tail!") and spends **two full turns** gathering it. The declared Ideal decides what lands:

| Ideal | Payload (of your Pokémon's max HP) | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Power** | 22% | −1 Sp. Def on your side |
| **Endurance** | 15% | Zekrom heals 8% of its own max HP |
| **Speed** | 16% | **Resolves at the start of the turn, before you act** |
| **Resolve** | 18% | **Cannot be broken** |

Add **4 points** to any of those at Conviction 5.

Your options:

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Hit it hard** (8%+ of its max HP in a turn) | **"ZEKROM'S FOCUS BREAKS!"** — the charge scatters and Conviction drops |
| **Switch out** | Does nothing — the lightning does not lose you, and switching forfeits your +1 anyway |
| **Under Resolve** | You cannot break it, and you cannot switch either |

It is a direct HP write, so **Substitute does not eat it.**

> **Grind Conviction below 3 and the Bolt Strike cycle never opens at all.** That is what the fight
> is really asking of you.

---

### Phase 0 — IDEALS (100% → 50%)

A random Ideal, declared, held for four turns, then re-picked. Conviction starts at 0 — **or at 1 if
you have already caught Reshiram**, because Zekrom recognises a trainer who has answered the other
question.

Learn the four counters here. They are the entire back half of the fight.

---

### Phase 1 — ABSOLUTE CONVICTION (50% → 15%)

> **"It will not change. Not now. Not for anything."**

Zekrom locks to the Ideal that **mirrors how you have been fighting** — pressure becomes Power,
outlasting becomes Endurance, speed becomes Speed, defiance becomes Resolve — and **it can never
change again.** Conviction is forced to 4.

That commitment is now *your* exploit. One counter, learned once, works for the rest of the fight.

Two other things change:

- **Zen Headbutt is replaced by Outrage.** It locks Zekrom in and confuses it afterwards — a real,
  waitable window.
- **The guard swings wider in both directions.** At DOUBT in this phase Zekrom is softer than it
  ever was in Phase 0. Grinding the meter down is worth more here than anywhere else in the fight.

---

### Phase 2 — IDEAL AWAKENING (15% → the end)

Conviction is pinned at **5** and the band stops moving. **Electric Terrain** goes up permanently —
Zekrom's own Electric moves are stronger, and nothing grounded can be put to sleep. Whatever status
you had on it is **burned off, once**, and **every stat stage on your side is wiped, once.**

Then it commits to **BOLT STRIKE: IDEAL** — one charge, **unbreakable under every Ideal**, for
**26%** of your Pokémon's max HP plus whatever extra its locked Ideal carries. When it resolves, the
phase is over either way.

---

### Phase 3 — SPENT (the catch window)

Its conviction finally falters. Everything the fight imposed comes off, Zekrom is restored to **8%
HP**, and **Poké Balls are unblocked** at a catch rate of 30. It cannot be knocked out while you are
throwing.

**This is the only point in the fight a ball works.** There is no earlier window.

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Read the declaration line.** It names the axis you have to fight on, and it holds for four
   turns — long enough to act on.
2. **Do the one thing that contradicts it**, every turn, until the meter falls.
3. **Do not set up.** Boosts feed the meter whether you meant them to or not.
4. **Do not switch.** It forfeits the turn's movement, it does not dodge a Bolt Strike, and under
   Resolve you cannot anyway.
5. **Grind Conviction under 3** and Bolt Strike never fires.
6. **In Phase 1, the locked Ideal is the whole answer.** Find its counter once and repeat it.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Sleep / paralysis lock | Works fine through Phases 0 and 1 — and under **Resolve** it is the counter. Electric Terrain blocks sleep from 15% on, plus one status clear |
| Switch-stalling / pivot chains | Resolve refuses it outright; every other Ideal makes it cost you the turn's meter movement |
| Toxic stall | The damage never ramps |
| OHKO moves, Endeavor, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Volt Absorb / Lightning Rod / Motor Drive / Levitate / Wonder Guard | Teravolt ignores your ability |
| Substitute stalling | Every scripted payload is a direct HP write |
| Setting up behind boosts | It is a **feeder** — the meter climbs while you do it |
| Grinding Phase 2 down to skip the final bolt | It survives at 1 HP and goes straight to the catch window |

Evasion stacking is only softly answered here — one scour, at Phase 2. Take the opening if you want
it; it is the least defended plan in the fight.
