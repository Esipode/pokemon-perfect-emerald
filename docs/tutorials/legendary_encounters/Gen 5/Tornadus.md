## Tornadus — The Cyclone Pokémon

Tornadus fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

> **The wind decides who is standing in front of it.**

Tornadus does not care which of your Pokémon it is fighting, because it picks. Every band of WIND
takes more of that choice away from you, and the whole fight is about buying back turns in which the
choice is yours again.

---

### WIND — 0 to 5

Announced every single time it moves, in both directions.

```
 0 Calm           nothing but the callout
 1 Rising
 2 Gale           gusts sweep the field every turn; forced switches begin (40% a turn)
 3 Tempestuous    the EYE OF THE STORM can form
 4 Roaring        gusts hurt more; the forced switch happens EVERY turn
 5 CYCLONE        it fires at the end of the turn, then Wind falls back to 2 (or 3 from Phase 1)

  +1  every turn, on its own — and +2 a turn from Phase 1
  +1  a Flying-type move resolved on the field — EITHER SIDE'S (once per turn)
  +1  you took LESS than 5% of Tornadus's max HP off this turn (it rode the attacks out)
  +1  a forced switch actually moved one of your Pokémon last turn
  +1  one of your Pokémon fainted
  −1  an Electric-type move landed on Tornadus
  −1  any weather stood on the field at the top of the turn
  −2  a Rock-type move landed on Tornadus
  −2/−3  the CYCLONE spent itself
```

The storm feeds itself faster than any single lever can drain it. Wind **is** meant to reach 5 and
the Cyclone **is** meant to fire — the levers buy you tempo, not suppression.

---

### THE THREE KEYS

Incarnate Tornadus is pure **Flying**, which gives you exactly three weaknesses. Each one does a
different job here, so bringing all three is the difference between steering the fight and riding it.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rock** | **Wind −2.** The stone breaks the wind's shape. Does *nothing at all* while the Eye stands |
| **Ice** | **Tornadus staggers** and loses its next action. No Wind change — and the Eye does not stop it |
| **Electric** | **Disruption.** Scatters the Eye, interrupts a gathering Hurricane, **Wind −1** — and an interrupt opens the sky |

Only one key counts per turn.

**None of them is mandatory.** A team with no Rock, Ice or Electric coverage still wins — it just
fights a longer, flatter version of the same battle and takes every gust on the chin.

---

### THE FOURTH LEVER: WEATHER

Any weather at all — rain, sun, sand, hail, snow — is a lever, and it is the only one that is not a
type. While a weather stands on the field:

- **Wind −1** at the top of every turn
- the **Eye cannot form**, and a standing Eye is **torn open**
- a **gathering Hurricane finds the sky already taken and disperses**

The **Cyclone is the only thing in the fight that clears weather.** A sky you take is worth several
turns, not one. This is the fight's real tug-of-war.

---

### THE EYE OF THE STORM

From Wind 3, in a clear sky, Tornadus opens a calm centre at its middle. While the Eye stands:

- **+2 Evasion, +1 Accuracy** — it is genuinely hard to hit, and Hurricane stops missing
- its guard goes **up**, not down
- it **ignores every Wind fall** — the Rock key and the weather tick both do nothing

It announces itself, keeps a line running every turn while it holds, and announces its passing. It
lasts two turns and cannot re-form for three.

Three ways to end it early: an **Electric** move scatters it, **any weather** tears it open, or you
simply wait it out.

---

### THE HURRICANE — Phase 1 and 2 only

From Wind 4, Tornadus begins gathering a Hurricane on a **three-turn countdown**, reprinted at the
top of every turn with a final warning on the last one.

Two answers:

| Answer | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **An Electric move lands on it** | Interrupted. **Wind −2**, Tornadus loses two actions, and **the sky opens** |
| **Any weather on the field** | The hurricane finds the sky taken and disperses |
| *(do nothing)* | It lands for **30%** of your active Pokémon's max HP (**40%** in Phase 2), drops two Speed stages, drags you out, and puts Wind straight back to 5 |

---

### DISPLACEMENT, THE GUST AND THE CYCLONE

These three run on Tornadus's schedule, not on its moves — so **a sleeping or frozen Tornadus still
does all of them.**

- **The gust**, from Wind 2, every turn: clears **entry hazards on both sides**, clears **your
  screens, Tailwind, Safeguard and Lucky Chant**, and chips you for 4% (7% from Wind 4). Nothing you
  build on the field survives.
- **Displacement**, from Wind 2: a 40% chance a turn at Wind 2–3, **certain from Wind 4**, and
  certain every turn through the Tempest. Your active Pokémon is dragged out for a random bench
  member. Setup sweeps, Substitute chains and a single tanked matchup all stop working.
- **The Cyclone**, at Wind 5: clears the weather, clears hazards and screens, chips you 12%, drags
  you out again, and drops Wind back to 2 (3 from Phase 1).

### THE PHASES

```
 Phase 0  STILL AIR     100–50%   guard 87.  Wind +1 a turn
 Phase 1  STORM FRONT    ≤ 50%    guard 90, Wind +2 a turn, and the HURRICANE unlocks at Wind 4
 Phase 2  TEMPEST        ≤ 15%    Therian forme, Bleakwind Storm, permanent Tailwind and +20% raw
                                  Speed, guard 93, Wind PINNED at 5, you are TRAPPED, and the gust
                                  and the drag happen every single turn
 Phase 3  GROUNDED        1 HP    every guard comes off and the ball is allowed
```

### THE OPEN SKY — the only real damage window

Landing an interrupt on a gathering Hurricane, or breaking the Tempest, **opens the sky**: Tornadus's
guard collapses to **70** for two turns (three from a Tempest break) and it loses two actions. That
flat 70 never gets worse as the fight goes on.

**The Tempest does not end on a timer.** It runs until **any one of the three keys** lands during it
— then the winds subside, the trap and the Tailwind come off, Wind drops to 0 and you get three
turns of open sky. Then the winds gather again and you have to earn the next window the same way.

A player who never lands a key never gets a window — and never strictly needs one. The Tempest's chip
is survivable; you just grind through 93% reduction instead of 70%.

---

### ANTI-ABUSE

| Thing you might try | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Sleep or freeze** | **Works completely, all fight, and is worth bringing.** But a sleeping Tornadus still gusts, still displaces and still cyclones. Sleep buys you its attacks, not its fight |
| Toxic stalling | Flat 1/16 a turn forever — the counter never ramps |
| OHKO moves, Super Fang, Pain Split, Destiny Bond, Perish Song | All blocked |
| Snarl, Icy Wind, Charm, Parting Shot | **Defiant.** Every stat drop is +2 Attack into a Hammer Arm |
| Swords Dance, Calm Mind, Substitute, Baton Pass chains | Displacement. The setup Pokémon is dragged out |
| Protect or Detect stalling | Displacement again, plus the gust and the Cyclone, neither of which Protect stops |
| Stealth Rock, Spikes, Reflect, Light Screen, Tailwind | The gust clears them every turn from Wind 2 |
| Earthquake, or hazards on its side | Pure Flying. Immune and irrelevant |

Its Phase 2 Therian forme trades Defiant for Regenerator, which does nothing on a boss that never
switches — but the stat-drop trap genuinely stops working there.

---

### THE CATCH

Poké Balls are refused for the entire fight. Take Tornadus to **1 HP** and it is grounded: the
damage reduction, the immunities, the effectiveness cap, the flat Toxic, the trap, the Tailwind and
the 1 HP guard all come off at once, the game tells you outright, and the ball works.
