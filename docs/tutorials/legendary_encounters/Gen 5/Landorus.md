## Landorus — The Abundance Pokémon

Landorus fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

> **Landorus does not defend itself — the land defends it. Break the land and you break Landorus.**

Two numbers run the fight, and both are announced whenever they change.

---

### STABILITY — 0 to 5

Everything Landorus gets, it gets from the ground it is standing on.

```
 0 Barren        nothing but the callout
 1 Stirring      +1 Def stage
 2 Grass         Grassy Terrain, with no timer
 3 Flourishing   EARTH PILLARS go live
 4 Abundant      Landorus heals 2% a turn, and TECTONIC SHIFT becomes available
 5 Continental   CONTINENTAL RUMBLE chips your side every turn

  +1  a Ground-type move resolved on the field — EITHER SIDE'S (once per turn)
  +1  one of your Pokémon fainted
  +1  every turn, on its own, from Phase 1
  −1  DESOLATION: a Fire- or Ice-type move landed on Landorus (once per turn)
```

Tiers **1 and 2 are taken back** when Stability falls — the Def stage goes, the terrain goes.
Tiers 3 to 5 simply switch off with the meter.

**The meter is the trap.** Landorus is Ground/Flying, so Ground coverage looks like nothing and Ice
looks like everything — but every Ground move that resolves, *including your own*, feeds the land,
and Fire is what holds it down even though Fire is not super effective here. A player who leads with
Earthquake fights a Continental Landorus for the whole battle.

---

### EARTH PILLARS — 0 to 3

Not a percentage: a **countable stack of turns your attack does not happen**.

- A standing pillar gives Landorus a **real Protect** for one turn — Feint, never-miss moves and
  contact punishment all behave exactly as they do against a real Protect — and then crumbles.
- A pillar **never stands two turns running**, and Landorus never hides behind a pillar and charges
  a Tectonic Shift on the same turn. One defensive turn or one telegraphed turn, never both.
- A pillar also falls to **any Fire or Ice hit**, and to **any turn in which you take 5% or more of
  Landorus's max HP**.
- New pillars come from reaching Stability 3, from the Therian transformation, from a landed
  Tectonic Shift, and from a rumble that finds the stack empty.

---

### The guard

```
 Phase 0  BARREN COUNTRY       88
 Phase 1  THERIAN POWER        90
 Phase 2  WRATH OF THE LAND    92

 reeling (a collapsed shift, or THE EARTH SETTLES): 80 / 82 / 84, for one turn
```

---

### TECTONIC SHIFT (Stability 4+)

- At the **start of a turn** Landorus drives its power into the ground — you see this before you
  choose a move.
- At the **end of that same turn** it resolves:
  - **Collapsed** — a **Fire or Ice move landed on Landorus during the charge**. The shift comes
    apart, every standing pillar falls, Landorus takes −1 Def / −1 Sp. Def, loses its next action,
    and its guard drops for a turn. Note the break condition: **damage does not stop this, only
    Desolation does.**
  - **Not collapsed** — the ground turns over. **Hazards on both sides, your screens and your stat
    stages are all scoured**, and your side takes **16% / 20% / 24%**, escalating with each quake.
    A fresh pillar comes up.

### CONTINENTAL RUMBLE (Stability 5)

The land chips your side for **7% every turn** the meter is held at 5, and throws up a new pillar
whenever none are standing. It is **not** a Phase 2 exclusive — let the meter reach 5 in Phase 0 and
it starts there.

### The moveset

| Slot | Phase 0 | Therian Power (50%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Earth Power | (same) |
| 2 | Rock Slide | (same) |
| 3 | Psychic | **Sandsear Storm** |
| 4 | Sludge Wave | (same) |

- **Sheer Force** is its ability: every one of those moves carries a secondary effect, so all of them
  hit harder and none of them ever debuffs you by accident.
- **Earth Power is special**, so Grassy Terrain — which Landorus raises itself — never weakens its
  own STAB the way it weakens Earthquake.
- **Rock Slide** answers the Ice and Flying answers the fight pushes you toward.

---

### Phase 1 — THERIAN POWER (50%)

- Landorus changes to its **Therian Forme**; slot 3 becomes **Sandsear Storm**
- Raw **Sp. Atk +15% / Def +10%**, written into the stats (a Haze will not remove it)
- **Gravity is raised permanently** — Flying-types, Levitate and Air Balloon lose their Ground
  immunity, and Fly, Bounce and evasion stop working
- **The weather is cleared at the top of every turn**, for the rest of the fight
- **Stability climbs +1 a turn on its own** from here, whatever you do

---

### Phase 2 — WRATH OF THE LAND (15%)

- Stability is forced to **5 and locked**. Desolation is **refused**, and Landorus says so — that
  line is the tell.
- The pillars refill, and the countdown to the ending starts: **six quakes** (shifts and rumbles
  counted together).

### THE EARTH SETTLES

After the sixth quake the land Landorus has been spending runs out. Stability falls to **0**, every
pillar comes down, Landorus takes **−2 Def / −2 Sp. Def**, loses its next action, and its guard
falls to **74**. The rumble stops for good.

**This is the largest window in the fight.** The meter starts climbing again from 0 immediately, so
spend it.

---

### The catch

At 1 HP the land lets go: Gravity, the terrain, the guard, the immunities, the type cap and Survive
all come off and Poké Balls unlock at a catch rate of 30. **This is the only point in the fight where
a ball works at all.** Catching Landorus also hands you the **Reveal Glass**.

---

### What does and does not work on it

| Plan | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Sleep / paralysis / freeze | Land and work completely, all fight — nothing is shed. But the Abundance heal and Continental Rumble are scripted, so they fire whether or not Landorus can act |
| Toxic | Lands, and the damage is flat — it never ramps |
| Ice coverage | The right answer twice over: it is the type cap's reason for existing here, and it knocks Stability down and breaks a pillar |
| Fire coverage | Not super effective, and still one of the two best moves in the fight — Desolation does not care about the type chart |
| Ground coverage (Earthquake, Bulldoze, Earth Power) | **Feeds the meter.** Your own Ground moves raise Stability, once per turn |
| A Flying-type or Levitate wall | Works until 50%. **Gravity** removes it, and Rock Slide, Psychic, Sludge Wave and Sandsear Storm cover the rest |
| Double Team / evasion | **Gravity** shuts it off from Phase 1 |
| Setting up (Swords Dance, Calm Mind) | **Tectonic Shift resets your stat stages** — and scours your screens and hazards with them |
| Weather teams | Cleared at the top of every turn from Phase 1 |
| Protect / Substitute stalling | The rumble and the shift are scripted percentages of your max HP and go straight through |
| Letting a Pokémon faint | **+1 Stability** every time |

---

### The short version

1. **Fight the land, not the Pokémon.** Stability is the whole fight; pillars are what actually stop
   your damage.
2. **Bring Fire or Ice.** Every hit knocks the meter down a level, breaks a pillar, and collapses a
   charging Tectonic Shift.
3. **Leave Earthquake at home.** Your own Ground moves feed the meter.
4. **A 25% turn splits a pillar** — big single hits are worth more than chip.
5. **Collapse the shift with Desolation, not with damage.** Damage does nothing to it.
6. **From 50% the meter climbs on its own**, so the fight gets faster the longer you take.
7. **Survive to the sixth quake in Phase 2** — the earth settles, and that is your window.
8. Save your ball. It does nothing until Landorus falls to 1 HP.
