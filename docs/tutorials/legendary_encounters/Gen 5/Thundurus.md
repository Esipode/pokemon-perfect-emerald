## Thundurus — The Bolt Strike Pokémon

Thundurus fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

> **CHARGE is a bomb, and you decide when it goes off.**

Every other legendary in this generation asks you to hold its meter **down**. This one is the
opposite: a suppressed Thundurus is safe, and it is also unkillable. The fight has to be *made* to
explode.

---

### CHARGE — 0 to 6

Announced every single time it moves, in both directions.

```
 0 Still         nothing but the callout
 1 Humming
 2 Crackling     LIGHTNING RODS go live
 3 Arcing        strikes hurt more and cost you a Speed stage; lightning starts falling on its own
 4 Blinding      the recurring static cue starts
 5 OVERCHARGED   raised critical-hit ratio, +15% Sp. Atk (raw), guard +2
 6 OVERLOAD      it discharges, Charge drops to 0, and its guard collapses to 70

  +1  an Electric-type move resolved on the field — EITHER SIDE'S (once per turn)
  +1  a rod stood through the whole turn
  +1  you took 5% or more of Thundurus's max HP off this turn (once per turn)
  +1  every turn, on its own, from Phase 1
  +2  a strike landed on you
  +2  one of your Pokémon fainted
  −1  a Rock- or Ice-type move landed on Thundurus (once per turn)
  −2  you broke a gathering strike, or earthed one on a Protect
```

**Charge 6 is the only real damage window the fight opens.** Keep the meter low all battle and you
will survive comfortably and get nowhere — 90% reduction plus the 1 HP guard means the grind never
ends. Rock and Ice are there so you can *choose* when the bomb goes off, not so you can defuse it.

---

### THE LIGHTNING ROD

From Charge 2, Thundurus drives a rod of lightning into **your** active Pokémon and starts a
**three-turn countdown**, reprinted at the top of every turn. This is the fight's signature mechanic
and it lives on your side of the field, not on the boss.

While a rod stands it feeds **Charge +1 a turn**, and it is what the bolt comes down on.

Three answers, and you always have three turns to pick one:

| Answer | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Protect the marked Pokémon** | The bolt earths harmlessly. Rod gone, **Charge −2** |
| **Switch it out** | The rod loses its ground. Rod gone, countdown cancelled, **Charge unchanged** |
| **Take 20% of its max HP off in one turn** | The discharge is disrupted. Rod gone, **Charge −2**, and **Thundurus loses its next action** |
| *(do nothing)* | The strike lands — see below |

After a rod is destroyed or spent, Thundurus needs **one clear turn** before it can drive another.

### When a strike lands

| Charge | Damage (% of your Pokémon's max HP) | Extra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 | 15% | — |
| 3–4 | 22% | −1 Speed |
| 5 | 30% | −1 Speed |
| 6 | 35% | −1 Speed |

Plus **Charge +2** — which is how you reach an Overload at all.

---

### The guard

```
 Phase 0  STATIC AIR           87   (89 at Charge 5+)
 Phase 1  THUNDERSTORM         90   (92 at Charge 5+)
 Phase 2  WRATH OF THE STORM   93   (95 at Charge 5+)

 reeling (any Overload): 80, flat, in every phase
```

The reeling number never gets worse as the fight goes on. Playing the mechanic correctly late is
worth exactly as much as playing it early.

---

### OVERLOAD (Charge 6)

At the end of the turn Charge reaches 6:

- Your whole side takes **30% of max HP**
- Thundurus tears itself for **8%**
- **Charge → 0**, every rod comes down
- Its guard falls to **80 for two full turns**, and it **loses its next action**

That is the window. Everything else in the fight is you deciding how fast to open it.

### THE STORM GOES SILENT (Phase 2, once)

The first Overload in Wrath of the Storm is bigger: the **rain and the tailwind come off**, the
window runs **three turns**, and Thundurus **loses two actions**. If it survives the silence, the
storm picks back up and Charge returns to **5** — the window is repeatable, but that particular beat
only happens once.

---

### Lightning out of the storm

From Phase 1, on any turn where **no rod stands** and Charge is **3 or higher**, a bolt falls on your
active Pokémon for **10%** at the end of the turn. Answering the countdown is never punished on top
of itself — the two never happen in the same turn.

### The moveset

| Slot | Phase 0 | Thunderstorm (50%) | Wrath of the Storm (15%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Thunderbolt | (same) | **Wildbolt Storm** |
| 2 | Thunder Wave | **Thunder** | (Thunder) |
| 3 | Sludge Wave | (same) | (same) |
| 4 | Focus Blast | (same) | (same) |

- **Prankster** is its ability in Phase 0, so **Thunder Wave has +1 priority** — it lands before your
  setup turn does.
- From Phase 1 the rain is permanent, so **Thunder never misses**.
- The Therian forme brings **Volt Absorb** with it, which replaces Prankster — and makes your own
  Electric moves heal it.

---

### Phase 1 — THUNDERSTORM (50%)

- **Permanent rain**
- Slot 2 becomes **Thunder**, raw **Sp. Atk +10%** (a Haze will not remove it)
- **Charge climbs +1 a turn on its own**
- **Lightning falls on the quiet turns**
- **Sleep and freeze are shaken off at the top of every turn**

### Phase 2 — WRATH OF THE STORM (15%)

- **Therian Forme**; slot 1 becomes **Wildbolt Storm**
- **Tailwind** plus raw **Speed +20%** — this is where "it moves first now" becomes literal
- **Charge is forced to 5**, and the rod cooldown is cleared, so a countdown is running almost every
  turn
- **Volt Absorb** — Electric coverage now heals it outright

---

### The catch

At 1 HP the storm lets go: the rain, the tailwind, the guard, the immunities, the type cap and the
1 HP guard all come off, and Poké Balls unlock at a catch rate of 20. **This is the only point in the
fight where a ball works at all.**

---

### What does and does not work on it

| Plan | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Sleep / freeze | Work **completely** in Phase 0 and are worth bringing. From Phase 1 the storm sheds them at the top of every turn, so they have to be re-applied |
| Paralysis | Sticks all fight — nothing sheds it |
| Toxic | Lands, and the damage is flat — it never ramps |
| Rock or Ice coverage | The suppression lever: **Charge −1** per turn, on top of being super effective |
| Electric coverage | **Feeds Charge**, and from Phase 2 Volt Absorb heals off it. The fight's central trap |
| Ground-type wall | Immune to its Electric STAB — and it carries **Sludge Wave** and **Focus Blast** for exactly that |
| Setting up (Dragon Dance, Calm Mind) | Priority **Thunder Wave** in Phase 0, a Speed stage gone with every landed strike, and every setup turn is a turn Charge climbs |
| Protect stalling | Protect is a *real* answer to the countdown (Charge −2), but Charge still climbs +1 a turn from Phase 1 and the storm strikes on the quiet turns |
| Switch-spamming every rod | Works, costs you the turn, and does **nothing** to Charge |
| Letting a Pokémon faint | **+2 Charge** every time |
| One huge hit to skip the ending | It cannot be taken below 1 HP until the storm is grounded |

---

### The short version

1. **The meter is not your enemy — the stalemate is.** A Thundurus at Charge 0 is one you cannot kill.
2. **Rock and Ice are a throttle, not a cure.** Use them to control *when* the Overload lands.
3. **Leave Electric coverage at home.** Every Electric move on the field, including yours, feeds it.
4. **Every rod gives you three turns.** Protect, switch, or hit for 20% — pick one before it resolves,
   and the two that cost you the most also cost it the most Charge.
5. **Let the last strike through.** Taking a hit at Charge 4 is what buys the Overload.
6. **The Overload window is two turns at guard 80** — have your damage ready before you trigger it.
7. **In Phase 2, survive to the silence.** Three turns, no rain, no tailwind, two lost actions.
8. Save your ball. It does nothing until Thundurus falls to 1 HP.
