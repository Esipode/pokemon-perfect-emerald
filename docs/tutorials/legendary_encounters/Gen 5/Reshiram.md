## Reshiram — The Vast White Pokémon

Reshiram fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

Its ability is **Turboblaze**. Your Pokémon's ability is ignored outright — Flash Fire, Levitate,
Water Absorb, Wonder Guard and every other defensive ability simply do not apply here.

> **Reshiram learns you.**

Everything you do is information, and information is the only currency in this fight. The more
Reshiram understands your strategy, the less anything you do to it matters.

---

### REVELATION — 0 to 5

One meter, announced in bands every time it crosses one, in **both** directions.

```
 0–1  UNREAD       it has nothing on you yet
 2–3  STUDIED      it is studying the way you fight
 4    UNDERSTOOD   it understands what you are trying to do
 5    KNOWN        it knows exactly what you will do
```

REVELATION owns Reshiram's damage reduction. Every band it climbs, your hits get worse.

**The meter changes direction halfway through the fight.** That reversal is the whole battle.

---

### Phase 0 — THE OBSERVER (100% → 50%)

Revelation **climbs on its own, every single turn.** You cannot win the meter here. You can only
lose it more slowly.

```
  +1  every turn, unconditionally
  +1  you repeated an attack category, or you switched
  −1  you took 10% or more of Reshiram's max HP off this turn
```

So a turn where you hit hard and changed category is a net **zero** — you held it still. Anything
less and it learns.

What turns on as the meter rises:

| Revelation | Turns on |
| --- | --- |
| **1+** | **REVEAL** — it reads you every turn |
| **2+** | **EXPOSE** — it strips what you built |
| **3+** | **TRUTH MARK** — it marks whoever is standing there |

**TRUTH FLAME** runs on a 4-turn cycle from the start of the fight. It is telegraphed one turn
ahead ("White flame gathers along Reshiram's tail…").

---

### REVEAL — the read

Every turn from Revelation 1, Reshiram announces what it thinks your Pokémon is about to do and
braces for exactly that:

| It says | It is expecting | It raises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "It reads the tension in your Pokémon's stance!" | a physical move | its **Defense** |
| "It reads the energy gathering around your Pokémon!" | a special move | its **Sp. Def** |
| "It sees your Pokémon is not going to attack!" | a status move | its **Sp. Atk** |

The last line is the important one. Reshiram does not defend against a setup turn — it **attacks
into it**. Setting up in front of this Pokémon is the single most expensive thing you can do.

It is the game's real prediction, not a script, so it is genuinely beatable. Read the line, then do
something else.

> **"It has seen that move before."** — you repeated a category. That is the +1.

---

### EXPOSE — the strip

From Revelation 2, on a **3-turn cooldown**, Reshiram takes:

- your screens (Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil, Safeguard, …)
- **every stat stage on your side**
- your entry hazards
- and then shorts out your held item for 3 turns (Embargo)

If it finds a bare field it takes nothing, spends no cooldown, and is **ready again next turn** —
so it is always waiting the moment you build something.

---

### TRUTH MARK — the brand

From Revelation 3, Reshiram marks your active Pokémon: **−1 Defense and −1 Sp. Def**, deepening to
**−3 / −3**.

It only deepens on a turn where **the marked Pokémon actually took HP off Reshiram**. The mark
tracks real threat, not whoever happens to be standing there.

- **Switching sheds it** — but Reshiram immediately re-marks the newcomer at depth 1.
- TRUTH FLAME and EXPOSE do **not** wipe it. Neither does your own Haze — it is re-applied.

---

### TRUTH FLAME — the scouring

Every 4 turns (3 from Phase 1), telegraphed one turn ahead:

- weather and terrain are gone
- screens on **both** sides are gone
- hazards on **both** sides are gone
- **stat stages on both sides** are reset — Reshiram's own Reveal stack included
- your side takes **8% of max HP**
- your side is **Heal Blocked for 3 turns**

It is symmetrical on purpose. It is not a punish, it is a reset — and it is the only thing keeping
Reshiram's own Reveal boosts from towering over a long fight.

---

### Phase 1 — THE TRUTH REVEALED (50% → 15%)

> **"Reshiram is certain of you now. Make it doubt."**

Revelation is forced to **5** and **stops climbing by itself.** The ledger flips:

```
  +1  you repeated an attack category, or you switched
  −1  you did NOT feed it (varied your attacks, stayed in)
  −1  you took 5% or more of Reshiram's max HP off this turn
```

From here the meter is **yours to grind down**, and every point you take off it is a point of
Reshiram's guard. At UNREAD in this phase Reshiram is softer than it ever was in Phase 0.

Two other things change:

- **Extrasensory is replaced by Fusion Flare.**
- **BLUE FLARE** opens up.

#### BLUE FLARE

While Revelation is **3 or higher**, Reshiram announces a target and spends **two full turns**
gathering blue fire before it lands. Your options:

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Hit it hard** (5%+ of its max HP in a turn) | The charge **breaks**, and Revelation drops |
| **Switch out** | Does nothing — the fire follows |
| **Let it land** | 25% / 35% / 45% of your Pokémon's max HP, by Revelation band |

It is a direct HP write, so **Substitute does not eat it.**

> **Grind Revelation below 3 and the Blue Flare cycle never opens at all.** That is what this phase
> is really asking of you.

---

### Phase 2 — THE TRUTH CANNOT BE HIDDEN (15% → the end)

Reshiram **stops defending and starts burning.** Its guard actually *drops* to a flat value — the
lowest it has held all fight outside a charge.

**WHITE LIGHT** goes up, permanently:

- **Gravity.** Every move's accuracy rises, the field is grounded, and Fly / Bounce / Sky Drop stop
  working. *(This hits your Flying-types and Levitate Pokémon too.)*
- **Mist on Reshiram's side.** You cannot lower its accuracy.
- **Every stat stage on your side is wiped at the top of every turn.** No evasion, no setup.
- Whatever status you had on Reshiram is **burned off, once.**

Then it commits to **TRUTH'S JUDGMENT** — one charge, **unbreakable**, for **55%** of your
Pokémon's max HP plus −2 Sp. Def. When it resolves, the phase is over either way.

---

### Phase 3 — SPENT (the catch window)

The flames fade. Everything the fight imposed comes off, Reshiram is restored to **8% HP**, and
**Poké Balls are unblocked** at a catch rate of 30. It cannot be knocked out while you are throwing.

**This is the only point in the fight a ball works.** There is no earlier window.

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Never use the same attack category twice in a row.** That is the meter, in one sentence.
2. **Never set up.** It reads the status turn and attacks into it.
3. **Hit for5%+ of its max HP every turn you can.** That is a −1 in *both* phases.
4. **Stay in.** Switching is a +1, it re-marks the newcomer, and it does not dodge a Blue Flare.
5. **In Phase 1, grind the meter under 3** and the Blue Flare cycle shuts off.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Sleep / paralysis lock | Works fine, actually — through both Phase 0 and Phase 1. WHITE LIGHT clears it **once**, at 15% |
| Toxic stall | The damage never ramps |
| OHKO moves, Endeavor, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x Rock weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Flash Fire / Levitate / Water Absorb / Wonder Guard | Turboblaze ignores your ability |
| Substitute stalling | Hyper Voice is sound-based, and every scripted payload is a direct HP write |
| Screens, hazards, Leftovers | EXPOSE, TRUTH FLAME and Heal Block |
| Evasion stacking | Scoured by TRUTH FLAME, then by Gravity plus a per-turn wipe |
| Grinding Phase 2 down to skip the Judgment | It survives at 1 HP and goes straight to the catch window |
