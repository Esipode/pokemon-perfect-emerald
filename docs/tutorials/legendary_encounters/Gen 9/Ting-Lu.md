## Ting-Lu — The Ruinous Vessel Pokémon

A boss that just... stands there and gets heavier. Every hit you land is stored as **Ruin Energy**,
and enough of it banked pushes its **Weight** up a notch — permanently. Heavier means it hits and
survives more, but its own footing gets worse too.

### RUIN ENERGY / WEIGHT — 0 to 6, only rises
Chip damage banks Ruin Energy. Every time enough banks, Weight climbs one stage (never falls) and
its Defense, Sp. Def and damage reduction with it — while its own Speed keeps dropping. Fast,
spread-out damage keeps Weight low longer than a few huge hits do.

### Phase 1 — Ruinous Ground (100% → 50%)
#### The ground splits
Ting-Lu telegraphs a Sinkhole under your Pokémon one turn before it opens. Switch out that same
turn and you walk away clean — stay in and it drops your Speed and chips a chunk of HP the turn
after.
#### The charge
Every few turns Ting-Lu gathers itself, then unleashes a straight Earthquake on whoever's in. The
telegraph gives you a turn to prepare — heal, switch, or just brace.
#### Ancient Earth
At Weight's highest tier the ground itself starts chipping every Pokémon on the field every turn,
Flying types included — this isn't a move, so nothing dodges it.

### Phase 2 — Ancient Mountain (50%, one-time)
Ting-Lu roots itself: Defense and Sp. Def spike, its guard hardens, and its own Speed craters even
further. That's the trade — it's much tankier, but it's also about as slow as it will ever be.

### Phase 3 — Mountain's Collapse (20% → the catch window)
Sinkholes come faster and hit harder. Poké Balls stay blocked until it staggers at 10% HP — the
moment it does, it dumps every last point of stored Ruin Energy into one final Earthquake, then its
Weight and hardening collapse for good. Throw once that quake resolves.

### What actually beats it
Fast, spread damage across many turns — it keeps Weight and its stats down. Switching the instant a
Sinkhole telegraphs is free damage avoidance. Bank a full team so the final Earthquake doesn't end
your run.

### What does not work
Stalling to "wait it out" — Weight only ever goes up, so the fight gets harder to out-damage the
longer it runs. A single big attacker also eats a full Earthquake at the worst possible Weight tier.
