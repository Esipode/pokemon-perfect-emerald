## Fezandipiti — The Retainer Pokémon

Fezandipiti loves being watched. It punishes you for looking away and rewards you for hitting back —
but it also punishes you for hitting it while it's showing off.

### GLAMOUR — its ego meter (0–6)

Rises when you let one of its attacks whiff, or when you pick "Focus" on its Beautiful Distraction
prompt. At 6 it goes full VANITY: its Defense and Sp. Defense crack open for a turn — punish it hard
right then. It settles back down afterward, not to zero.

### PERFUME — stacks on whoever's in

Every hit Fezandipiti lands on your active Pokémon stacks Perfume. Switch out or dodge one of its
hits to clear it. Let it reach 3 stacks and TOXIC CHARM locks in — a real per-turn tax until you
clear it.

### Phase 1 — Debut (100–51% HP)

- Beautiful Distraction: it asks if you want to Focus or Ignore. Ignoring three times back to back
  makes it lash out for real damage.
- Occasionally it steals the Spotlight — a self-buff, nothing more. Let it fade.

### Phase 2 — Perfect Beauty (≤50% HP)

Everything above still runs, plus:
- Glamour now climbs on its own, turn after turn.
- Perfume builds twice as fast off its hits.
- POISONOUS KISS marks your active Pokémon — a recurring tax that breaks if you land a big enough
  hit on Fezandipiti yourself.

### Phase 3 — Jealous Beauty (≤20% HP)

It speeds up. Three Ignores in a row no longer just hurts you — it collapses Fezandipiti's own
Glamour and cracks its guard open, a real opening to press.

### The catch window

At 10% HP, regardless of phase, it opens up — "now is the moment."

### What actually beats it

Keep attacking it. A dodged hit or a whiffed attack feeds its ego, so consistency matters more than
one big swing. Switch or dodge to shed Perfume before Toxic Charm locks in. Pick "Focus" sparingly —
it's honest but it feeds Glamour on purpose.

### What does not work

Stalling it out. Evasion and passive play read as "getting away with it" and only fill the Glamour
meter faster.
