## Chien-Pao — The Ruinous Sword Pokémon

Chien-Pao hunts. Everything it does gets faster and harder as it grows more aggressive - and it
grows more aggressive when you let it dictate the pace.

### MOMENTUM — 0 to 6

Climbs on: attacking, moving first, chaining hits, finding an opening, switching out.
Drops on: breaking a Sword Dash, interrupting a Ruinous Blade.
The field itself gets colder as Momentum climbs - watch for the tone shift around 3 and 5.

### Phase 1 — Hunt (100% → 50%)

Every hit you take chips a flat amount off your HP - that never stops.

#### Sword Dash
At Momentum 4+, Chien-Pao vanishes into the snow. If its next attack lands, the payoff is heavy.
Break it with Protect or by forcing a miss - a broken Dash costs it Momentum.

### Phase 2 — Whiteout (50% → 15%)

Chien-Pao's Speed spikes hard and stays up.

#### Ruinous Blade
At Momentum 5+, the broken swords start glowing. Land a real hit on Chien-Pao before it releases
and the attack scatters, costing it Momentum. Let it finish and it tears through your side's
screens on the way to a heavy hit.

#### Peak Momentum
At Momentum 6 in this phase, a landed hit spikes with bonus damage - but the spike burns two points
of Momentum, so it can't happen back-to-back.

### Phase 3 — Avalanche (≤15%, the catch window at ≤10%)

Momentum locks at maximum and every landed hit chains into the next, hitting harder the longer the
chain runs. Miss once and the chain resets to zero. At 10% HP, Chien-Pao staggers - now is the
moment, and Poké Balls finally work.

### What actually beats it

Keep Momentum low. Play defensively when it's not attacking outright, break every Sword Dash and
Ruinous Blade you see telegraphed, and don't let a long clean hit-chain build in the final phase.

### What does not work

Waiting it out passively - the Hunted chip never stops, so stalling only costs you HP for free.
Poké Balls before 10% HP - they're blocked outright until then.
