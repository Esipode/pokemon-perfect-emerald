## Wo-Chien — The Ruinous Tablet Pokemon

A rotting stone tablet that punishes patience. Every turn you stall, every point of damage you take,
and every point of HP you heal feeds a decay that never reverses. The fastest, most aggressive line
through this fight is also the safest one.

### DECAY — 0 to 6, never falls
Climbs from: a turn passing, Wo-Chien's Grass moves or draining hitting you, you losing HP, you
healing, and any faint. It drives the field through four stages and never goes back down.

### Phase 0 — Spreading Rot (100% → 50%)
#### The field withers, then rots
As Decay climbs the field turns from Normal to Withered to Rotting to Dead. Withered and up brings
two new dangers online (below). Rotting and Dead shut off healing outright for your whole team.
#### Ruinous Vines
Vines wrap your active Pokemon once the field withers, draining it and healing Wo-Chien every turn.
Break them with a real haymaker — land a hit for at least 20% of Wo-Chien's max HP in one turn — or
they keep draining. Breaking them also bursts a spore cloud that costs the breaker some HP, so don't
chip at them, commit to the swing.
#### Ruin Mark
Once the field withers, a curse mark settles on whoever is out. That Pokemon can't be healed at all,
and every hit Wo-Chien lands on it does extra damage. If the marked Pokemon faints, Decay spikes hard
— keep it alive and keep its HP up front-of-mind.

### Phase 1 — Rotting Forest (50% → 15%)
Decay is forced fully rotten the instant you cross 50%. Wo-Chien starts healing itself every turn.
Vines and the Ruin Mark keep cycling. Once in this phase, Wo-Chien channels RUINOUS DRAIN — you get
that one turn to land another 20%-of-max-HP haymaker and interrupt it, or it heals a big chunk back.

### Phase 2 — Ruinous Leaves (15% and below, the catch window)
The field is fully Dead. Vines and the Ruin Mark drop away, but the field keeps chipping your HP
every turn, worse each turn it goes on. Poke Balls unlock here — Wo-Chien is fading and this is your
window.

### What actually beats it
Play fast and heavy. Go for the haymaker whenever Wo-Chien gives you the opening — it breaks vines,
interrupts the drain channel, and ends the fight faster, which is the one thing Decay can't punish.
Keep whoever is marked topped up and alive rather than healing reactively once they're already low.

### What does not work
Stalling, chip damage, and leaning on healing. All three feed Decay directly, and Decay only ever
gets worse for you.
