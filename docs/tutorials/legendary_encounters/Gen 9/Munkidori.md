## Munkidori — The Manipulative Trickster Pokémon

Munkidori reads you. Every time it guesses right, it learns more; every time you break the
pattern, it loses ground. Stop feeding it information.

### INFLUENCE — 0 to 6

Rises when Munkidori's read pays off: it calls your move category correctly, you fall for its
bluffed tell, a Toxic Thought rotation runs its full course, or you use the move category it just
warned against. Falls when you break a read: it guesses wrong, or you see through a bluff. At 6 it
goes **OVERCONFIDENT** — its guard drops.

### Phase 1 — Opening (100% → 50%)

#### Mind Games
Each turn Munkidori tries to read your move category. Land a hit in the category it expected and
its read pays off. Vary what you throw at it.

#### Toxic Thought
A real Toxic status, re-themed. It runs a fixed 4-turn rotation: lands, clouds your judgment
(Attack/Sp. Atk drop), sits, then clears (stats return, and riding it out to the end feeds
Munkidori's read).

#### The bluffed tell
Before you choose a move, Munkidori tips its hand — real or fake. Protect against a real tell and
you dodge nothing; Protect against a fake one and you've just shown your hand.

### Phase 2 — Psychic Control (50% → 20%)

Munkidori starts calling out a move category before you act. Use that category anyway and it gets
reflected back as a chip hit on your own Pokémon. At max Influence here it goes **OVERCONFIDENT** —
punish the opening, then watch Influence fall back below 6 to close it again.

### Phase 3 — Mind Break (≤20%, the catch window)

Influence only drops on a clean counter now — it never fades on its own — and Toxic Thought's stat
swing doubles. The moment Influence maxes out again here, Munkidori gathers everything it's read
into one crushing burst, then its guard collapses completely. That's the moment to throw a ball.

### What actually beats it

- Mix your move categories instead of leaning on one.
- Don't Protect blind — read whether the tell feels real.
- Once it calls out a category, avoid it.
- Let Toxic Thought's rotation run rather than fighting it.

### What does not work

- Spamming Protect — a fake tell punishes it directly.
- Repeating one move category — Command Hijack punishes it directly.
- Stalling — Toxic Thought's damage stays flat and expires on its own.
