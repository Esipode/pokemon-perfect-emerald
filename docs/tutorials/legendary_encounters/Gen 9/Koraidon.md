## Koraidon — The Winged King Pokémon

Koraidon fights to a rhythm. Every landed hit, every read of your patterns, and every turn its Sun
holds feeds that rhythm - and the stronger it gets, the bigger the payload it can unleash on you.
Starve the rhythm and the fight stays survivable. Feed it and Koraidon starts hitting back harder
than anything else in the game.

### MOMENTUM — 0 to 6

Climbs when Koraidon's own attack lands. Falls when it misses, gets blocked, takes a status
effect, or loses its Sun. At 4+ it can gather a PRIMAL ROAR; at 6 (and past 50% HP down) it can
unleash COLLISION. Landing a hit on Koraidon while it channels or charges breaks the attempt
outright - and costs it Momentum for trying.

### Phase 0 — Radiant Predator (100% → 50%)

#### Reading you
Koraidon reads your last move. Throw the same TYPE of attack twice in a row and it answers in
kind, quickening itself. Any other physical hit makes it brace, hardening its Defense. Switch
Pokémon and it reads the newcomer the same way. Vary your attacks and switch sparingly to stay
unpredictable.

#### Primal Roar
At Momentum 4+, Koraidon gathers a Roar at the start of a turn. Hit it before the turn ends and
the Roar shatters, costing it Momentum. Let it complete and its Sun burns hotter, and your own
stat changes are wiped.

### Phase 1 — Apex Predator (≤ 50%)

Koraidon's Attack and Speed rise on entry. COLLISION is live now: at Momentum 6, it spends the
whole turn charging a direct hit on your active Pokémon. Protect, or knock its Momentum below 6
before the turn ends, and the charge breaks (again costing it Momentum). Let it land and it's a
heavy hit scaled by how strong its Sun has grown.

### Phase 2 — Primal Instinct (≤ 20%, the catch window)

Koraidon surges again - Speed and Attack climb further, but its accuracy starts to slip.
COLLISION becomes COLLISION COURSE: costlier, same rules to break it. At 10% HP it finally slows
down - that's the signal to start throwing Poké Balls.

### What actually beats it

- Vary your move types and switch only when you mean it - Koraidon's reads reward unpredictability.
- Deny it Momentum: block its hits, poison or paralyze it, or just outlast its Sun.
- Punish the charge-up beats. A Roar or a Collision announces itself before it resolves - hit
  Koraidon during that window and the payload never lands.
- Balls do nothing until 10% HP. Don't waste them earlier.

### What does not work

- Stalling passively. A move that never lands never denies Momentum - Koraidon keeps its rhythm
  either way unless you're actively interrupting or starving it.
- Repeating the same attack type over and over. That's exactly what it's watching for.
