## Chi-Yu — The Ruinous Bead Pokémon

A boss that turns everything into fuel for its own fire. Every Fire move it lands, every burn it
inflicts, and every turn you let its windups finish feeds one meter — and that meter decides how
hard it hits back.

### FLAME — 0 to 6, both directions
Warm (2-3), Scorched (4-5), Inferno (6). Hitting Chi-Yu hard and fast keeps Flame down. Letting a
burn sit three turns straight, or letting a windup complete, sends it climbing. At Scorched or
higher, your own healing is blocked every turn.

### Phase 1 — 100% → 50%
#### Burning Beads
A big single hit in one turn shatters a Bead — it explodes back at you, but Chi-Yu banked one less
of them.
#### Greedy Flame
Every so often Chi-Yu draws inward for two turns. Land a big hit during the windup and it breaks
for nothing; let it finish and Chi-Yu heals, buffs itself, and Flame spikes.
#### The burn that lingers
Keep a burn on your active Pokémon for three turns running and the flames spread — a big jump in
Flame. Curing it, or switching it off, resets the count.

### Phase 2 — Wildfire (≤50% HP)
The field is on fire now. Beads appear on their own every turn and Flame climbs passively on top
of everything above. Flame maxing out arms a one-turn telegraph, then Firestorm burns the whole
field.

### Phase 3 — Consuming Ruin (≤15% HP, the catch window opens at ≤10%)
Chi-Yu commits fully. One turn later, Ruinous Pyre devours every Bead it still has and unleashes
damage scaled by how much was banked, then the fire dies down to embers for good. Poké Balls open
at 10% HP.

### What actually beats it
Burst damage early keeps Beads from stacking and denies Greedy Flame its payoff. Cure or switch
off a burn before the third turn. Save your big hit for a Greedy Flame windup.

### What does not work
Stalling behind healing once Flame is Scorched or higher — it's blocked every turn. Letting Flame
sit at 6 for long; Firestorm is coming regardless.
