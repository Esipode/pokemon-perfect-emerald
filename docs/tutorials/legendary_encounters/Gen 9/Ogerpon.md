## Ogerpon — The Masked Guardian Pokémon

Ogerpon reads how you fight it. Lean on physical attacks and it answers with a mask built to shrug
them off; lean on special attacks and it answers with something else. Land a big hit right after it
switches masks, though, and the mask breaks before it can do anything with it.

### Phase 0 — masks come and go (100% → 50%)

Ogerpon studies you every turn while it wears the Teal Mask. Land a hit and its focus sharpens
further. Once it's studied you enough, it dons whichever mask answers your attacks best — lean
physical and it turns to stone; lean anything else and it tries on Water or Fire.

#### The break window

For two turns right after a mask goes up, landing 5%+ of Ogerpon's max HP in that stretch cracks
the mask and sends it back to Teal before the mask's own attack goes off.

#### Wellspring / Hearthflame / Cornerstone

Each masked stance builds its own pressure from staying in the fight, and unleashes something once
it's ready: Wellspring heals a chunk of HP back, Hearthflame blasts you for real damage, and
Cornerstone holds two shield charges that soften every hit it eats — break both charges and the
shield shatters back at you.

### Phase 1 — Guardian's Resolve (≤50%)

Ogerpon stops reacting and starts cycling through every mask on a clock: Wellspring, Hearthflame,
Cornerstone, back to Teal, and around again — each lap a little faster than the last. The break
window is still live on every mask it puts on; cracking one skips straight to the next stance
instead of waiting out the clock.

### Phase 2 — Unyielding (≤10%)

Whatever mask Ogerpon is wearing fires its signature attack one last time, then it settles back to
Teal and unleashes a Masked Strike — bigger the more masks it showed you during the fight. After
that its guard drops for good.

#### The catch window

Once the Masked Strike lands, Poké Balls unlock.

### What actually beats it

Mix your damage between physical and special so it can't commit to one mask, and hit hard the
instant a new mask appears — that two-turn window is the only time you can stop a mask before it
pays off. Against Cornerstone, keep swinging; the shield only has two charges.

### What does not work

Stalling for the mask timer to run out on its own — Mask Energy builds on its own every turn
whether you attack or not. Turtling against Cornerstone doesn't dodge the shockwave either; it
fires the moment the shield breaks, not on a move you can see coming.
