## Terapagos — The Terastal Pokémon

Terapagos hardens against whatever keeps hitting it. Vary your attack types, and punish its
periodic exposed windows with a strong hit before its stored charge becomes a last-stand attack.

### STELLAR ENERGY — how much it has banked

Repeating one attack type lets Terapagos harden against it and feeds this charge. Landing a big hit
during an exposed window strips it back down. It only ever shows up in flavor text, but the more
of it Terapagos has banked when it reaches its last stand, the scarier that finale reads.

### Phase 0 — Dormant (100% → 67%)

Terapagos studies every type you throw at it. Keep hitting it with the same type and it hardens
against that type and banks more charge; switch it up and it can't.

### Phase 1 — Terastal (66% → 34%)

Terapagos cracks its shell open. A crystal periodically breaks the surface — land a solid hit on
it before it sinks back down to shatter it and strip its charge. Miss the window and it keeps what
it had.

### Phase 2 — Stellar Cataclysm (33% → 0%)

Terapagos gathers every type it knows. Hit it with the type it's currently channeling several
times in a row and it overloads, faltering briefly. Terastallizing your own Pokémon while its
crown burns feeds it your energy instead — safe outside that window, costly inside it. The crown
itself opens the same way the Phase 1 crystal did, and breaking it strips a bigger chunk of charge.

#### The catch window

Once Terapagos drops to 10% HP, its crown dims and Poké Balls unlock.

### What actually beats it

Rotate your attack types so nothing ever hardens for long, and watch for the crystal/crown windows
— they're the best chances to cut its stored charge down before the fight ends.

### What does not work

Spamming one move type. It hardens fast, and every hardened hit is one that stops mattering.
