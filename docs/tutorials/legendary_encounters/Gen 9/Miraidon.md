## Miraidon — The Iron Serpent Pokémon

Everything you do is information. Repeat yourself, sit on a resisted hit, or let a big shot land
clean, and Miraidon's Analysis Network gets sharper. Break the pattern and it starves.

### ANALYSIS — 0 to 6

Climbs from: hitting Miraidon with a resisted type (Electric, Dragon, Steel, Flying, Grass), using
the same move category twice in a row, and correctly guessing what Miraidon calls at 3+ (below).
Falls from: knocking a Node offline (see Phase 1), and guessing wrong once Miraidon starts calling
its shot.

At Analysis 3+, Miraidon announces "MIRAIDON IS CALCULATING!" — it has committed to reading
physical, special, or status this turn. Guess along with it (attack it with what it *doesn't*
expect) and it punishes you hard for landing a hit it called correctly; misjudge and its Analysis
drops instead.

### Phase 0 — Analysis Network (100% → 50%)

#### Nodes and the destroy window

Miraidon starts with one active Electric Node. Land 5%+ of its max HP in a single hit and a Node
goes offline — Analysis takes a hit too. This is your one lever against a meter that otherwise only
climbs. Every other 3-4 turns Miraidon runs a SCAN once Analysis reaches 2, buffing whichever of
Attack/Sp. Attack it last got hit with for a few turns.

### Phase 2 — Future Engine (50% → 20%)

Nodes regenerate to 2. Once Analysis caps at 6 with 2+ Nodes still up, Miraidon Overclocks: +2
Speed and a burn of residual damage each turn for three turns, eating a Node every tick. Run it out
of Nodes and the Overclock collapses on its own, Speed and all.

### Phase 3 — Paradox Collapse (below 20%)

Analysis and Nodes both max out on entry. Guess wrong three times here and Miraidon suffers a
System Failure: its type-effectiveness cap and Electric Terrain both drop for three turns — the one
window a real type advantage hits at full force.

At 15% HP, Paradox Drive fires once: a heavy hit scaled by how much Analysis and how many Nodes
survived, then every meter resets to zero.

#### The catch window

At 10% HP, Poké Balls unlock.

### What actually beats it

Vary your move category turn to turn, and don't let a hit sit resisted. Once Miraidon starts
calling its shot, answer with what it *isn't* expecting. Landing a real haymaker (5%+ in one hit)
is worth the Node it costs you late — a stripped-down Miraidon hits softer at 15%.

### What does not work

Spamming one move type or category feeds Analysis directly and gets read once it's calling shots.
Chip damage under 5% never touches a Node.
