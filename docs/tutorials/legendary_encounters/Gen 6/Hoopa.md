## Hoopa — The Mischief Pokémon

Hoopa fights from inside its own rings: immune to OHKO, fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves,
incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, flat Toxic damage, and a damage reduction that never
drops below 60%. Its ability is **Magician**.

> **The rings always warn you first. What you do with that warning is the whole fight.**

---

### PORTAL ENERGY — 0 to 6

```
 0–1  guard 60–65%   thin, gutters
 2–4  guard 70–80%   turns slowly
 5–6  guard 85–90%   crowds the air, almost nothing gets through
```

Energy drifts up on its own between rings and drops when a ring lands cleanly. **Protecting when a
ring resolves shatters it instead** — Hoopa loses 2 Energy (a much bigger drop than a landed ring
costs) and takes a real chunk of damage. That's the main way to force the guard down.

Protect isn't free insurance, though:

- **Hyperspace Hole and Hyperspace Fury go through Protect outright.** Hoopa says so when it happens.
- Protect used turn after turn stops working as reliably, the same as it always does.
- Rings resolve every other turn — burn Protect on the wrong turn and it isn't there when you need it.

---

### Phase 0 — CONFINED (100% → 50%)

Energy opens at 3 (75% guard). A ring is always telegraphed one full turn before it closes, and the
order is fixed and learnable: **Hands → Thieves → Teeth**, repeating.

| Ring | Telegraph | What it does if it lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hands** | grasping hands ring opens | 6% chip, and you lose your next action |
| **Thieves** | ring opens over your side | strips your screens, hazards and stat stages, then Hoopa shields itself — the *next* Thieves ring instead returns everything as a burning field |
| **Teeth** | ring turns to face Hoopa | Hoopa shields itself first, then 10% chip |

Protecting on the turn any of these resolves shatters the ring instead of letting it land.

---

### Phase 1 — UNBOUND (50% → 15%)

Hoopa Unbound, and Energy jumps straight to 6 (90% guard). **Hyperspace** opens the phase: for
three turns the array is locked (Energy frozen, your healing sealed) while distortion holds. Then
it **collapses** — Energy drops to 0 (60% guard) and healing comes back. That collapse is the
biggest damage window in the fight.

Ring of Chains joins the cycle after Hyperspace opens: **Hands → Thieves → Teeth → Chains**. Chains
traps you and tightens for 7% a turn over three turns, then closes for 15% and releases you.

Hyperspace Fury replaces Hyperspace Hole as Hoopa's signature move here — same Protect-piercing
rule applies.

---

### Phase 2 — THE SIX RINGS (15% → the end)

Normal ring mechanics stop. Hoopa can't be knocked out here — six portals telegraph one turn ahead
and fire in sequence, each one bigger than the last (roughly 8% up to 18% of your Pokémon's max
HP). Switching and healing are open the entire time, so use them.

Once the sixth portal fires, the rings fail. **Now is the moment.**

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Protect on the turn a ring resolves.** That's the real counter — it costs Hoopa 2 Energy and
   real HP, far more than the ring itself would have cost you.
2. **Don't Protect against Hyperspace Hole/Fury.** It goes through anyway, and the ring counter
   doesn't apply to it.
3. **Grind Energy down early.** A weakly-guarded Hoopa in Phase 0 stays weak into Phase 1's opening.
4. **Play through the Hyperspace collapse.** It's a guaranteed 60%-guard window — bring your best
   hit for it.
5. **In the Six Rings, just switch and heal.** There's no guard mechanic left to manage — survive
   six hits and the catch window opens.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Protect every single turn | Decays with repeated use, and does nothing against Hyperspace Hole/Fury |
| OHKO moves, fixed-damage, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Toxic stall | The damage never ramps |
| Waiting out Hyperspace | Healing is sealed for those three turns |
| Trying to escape or switch during Chains | You're trapped until it releases you |
