## Yveltal — The Destruction Pokémon

Every point of damage dealt or denied anywhere feeds Yveltal's DESTRUCTION. The higher it climbs,
the harder Yveltal is to hurt and the harder it hits - until it maxes out, detonates, and burns
itself back to zero. That detonation is your one real opening.

### DESTRUCTION — 0 to 10

Rises: whenever Yveltal lands a Dark or Flying hit, whenever its Life Drain steals from you, when a
screen/hazard you set up gets stripped, when its Destruction Mark detonates, and when Oblivion Wing
lands. In Phase 1 it also climbs any turn your own Pokémon loses HP at all - so chip damage feeds it
too. A line calls out the current band whenever it changes, and a short one repeats while it's above
baseline.

### Phase 0 — PREDATION (100% → 51%)

#### LIFE DRAIN
Periodically, Yveltal steals a boosted Attack or Sp. Atk stage straight off your active Pokémon and
takes some HP with it. Don't stack offensive boosts on the Pokémon facing it - Yveltal will just take
them.

#### DESTRUCTION MARK
Landing a hit on Yveltal marks you for destruction. The mark ticks bigger damage every turn it's
held, and if it's not cleared or outlasted in time it detonates for a devastating final hit before
resetting. Switching does not clear it - the mark follows whichever Pokémon you have out.

#### DESTRUCTION PULSE
Screens and hazards on your side get stripped on sight, and stripping them feeds Destruction. Don't
bother stalling behind Reflect/Light Screen/Spikes - it costs you the setup AND powers Yveltal up.

### Phase 1 — WINGS OF DESTRUCTION (50% → 21%)

Healing is blocked on your side for the rest of the fight (re-applied every turn). Oblivion Wing
telegraphs over several turns - when it lands, it hits hard (scaled to how high Destruction is),
heals Yveltal, and re-marks your Pokémon. You can't out-stall this phase; every turn you take damage
feeds the meter that makes Oblivion Wing hit harder.

### Phase 2 — DESTRUCTION INCARNATE (20% → the catch window)

Destruction locks at its maximum for the rest of the fight - Yveltal is as tough and as hard-hitting
as it gets. It begins charging The Destruction of All, a three-turn countdown to a massive hit on
your whole team. Hit Yveltal hard enough during the charge and you interrupt it early; otherwise it
resolves in full. Either way, the charge's end is Yveltal's detonation: Destruction drops to zero,
its guard collapses, and the catch window opens. Now is the moment - throw the Ball.

### What actually beats it

Playing patiently in Phase 0 - avoid trading boosts Life Drain can steal, and don't lean on
screens/hazards. Clearing or outlasting Destruction Mark instead of tanking it to detonation. In
Phase 1, minimizing HP loss on your active Pokémon rather than absorbing chip damage. In Phase 2,
piling damage onto Yveltal during the charge to force the early interrupt - the fight rewards
offense there, not stalling.

### What does not work

OHKOs, fixed-damage moves, Pain Split, Destiny Bond/Perish Song - all blocked outright. Racing
Destruction down by damaging Yveltal in Phase 2 - it's locked at maximum and can't be lowered until
the charge resolves. Poké Balls do nothing until the detonation.
