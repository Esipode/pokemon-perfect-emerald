## Diancie — The Jewel Pokémon

Diancie doesn't dodge your attacks. It grows a crystal lattice and lets them break against it. The
lattice only cracks under a real blow — chip damage never adds up.

### FACETS — 0 to 6

Each facet makes Diancie's guard stronger. Facets grow on their own every few turns, whenever
Diancie lands a Rock or Fairy move, and whenever one of your Pokémon faints. The only way to break
one is to deal **5% or more of Diancie's max HP in a single turn** — anything less does nothing to
the lattice, no matter how many turns you spend chipping.

- More facets = more guard, and (from the second phase on) more healing for Diancie.
- At 4+ facets, Diancie can encase you: trapped, no healing items or moves, slower, and hitting
  softer for a few turns.
- At high facets, Diancie starts charging **Diamond Storm**. Its guard drops while charging — this
  is your best window to land a big hit and break a facet before the storm goes off. Every facet you
  break during the charge shrinks the storm; break enough and it collapses on Diancie instead.

### Phase 1 — Crystal Growth (100% → 70%)

Facets grow every 3 turns. Survive the encasing, and swing hard when the storm starts charging.

### Phase 2 — Jewel of Life (70% → 40%)

The field turns to crystal (permanent Misty Terrain). Facets now grow every 2 turns and heal
Diancie back some HP each turn. The storm threshold drops, so it charges sooner.

### Phase 3 — Mega Diancie (40% → 10%)

Diancie Mega Evolves. Facets snap to full and a bigger storm — Cataclysm — starts charging right
away. Bring your best burst.

### Phase 4 — Spent (the catch window)

Diancie's lattice goes dark and its guard collapses. Poké Balls only work from here — throw away.

### What actually beats it

Big, single hits. A move that deals 5%+ of Diancie's health in one shot breaks a facet outright.
Save your hardest attack for when the storm is charging — the guard drop makes it easier to land a
real break, and doing so shrinks or collapses the storm before it fires.

### What does not work

Repeated small hits never add up — the lattice only reads damage dealt in the same turn. Stalling
with poison or burn just feeds Diancie more time to grow facets and doesn't threaten it at all.
