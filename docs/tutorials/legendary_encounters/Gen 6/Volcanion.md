## Volcanion — The Steam Pokémon

Volcanion is barely dentable at rest. It vents its own built-up pressure the moment nothing stops
it - but a vent raises a field of its own steam, and Volcanion can't vent while that field still
stands. Push it hard enough during that window and it overloads, and that overload is your real
shot at it.

### STEAM PRESSURE — 0 to 10

Climbs every turn on its own, faster in later phases. Climbs faster still if Volcanion attacks you
with a Fire or Water move, if you hit it with a Water move, or if you land a big hit (4%+ of its
health) in a single turn. Only a vent or an overload brings it back down - it never falls on its
own. LOW / HIGH / CRITICAL bands are called out as they change; the meter also hisses while it's
elevated. Oddly, the guard gets *weaker* the higher Pressure climbs - Volcanion is straining, not
hardening.

### Phase 0 — PRESSURE (100% → 50%)

Volcanion vents once Pressure hits 3 and no field is up: a scripted hit on you, a four-turn fog
that dulls both sides' accuracy, and a burning field on your side that chips you every turn. It
also raises its own Special Attack while a field stands.

While a field is up, Volcanion cannot vent again. If Pressure reaches 10 during those four turns,
it overloads: PRESSURE RELEASE blows the field away, costs Volcanion its next turn, and drops its
guard hard for three turns. That's your window - pile on damage before it clamps back down.

Watch for Water Absorb: hitting Volcanion with a Water move spikes Pressure fast, but it also
heals Volcanion a quarter of its health. One well-timed Water hit near Pressure 8 can force an
early overload; spamming Water moves just keeps healing it.

### Phase 1 — SUPERHEATED (50% → 20%)

Pressure now climbs +2 a turn instead of +1, and Volcanion can stack a *second* field on top of
the first for even more Special Attack. WATER INJECTION also starts here: every so often Volcanion
draws in water on its own, then two turns later that water flashes to steam for another spike -
watch for the warning line so you know it's coming.

### Phase 2 — MELTDOWN (20% and below, the catch window)

Volcanion cannot vent at all anymore - every turn its Pressure only goes up, +3 a turn, and it now
carries STEAM ERUPTION as a signature move. There's no way to stop the meter from reaching 10; it's
only a matter of turns. When it does, ERUPTION fires: a heavy hit to you, everything reset, and
Volcanion left wide open. Poké Balls only work from this point on - "now is the moment."

### What actually beats it

Chip it steadily to force vents, then push Pressure hard once a field is up to force the overload.
Time a single Water hit for the final push if you need it, but don't lean on it. Once it overloads,
unload everything - that three-turn window is when your damage actually counts.

### What does not work

Balls before the finale. Spamming Water moves - the healing outpaces the Pressure you're adding.
Waiting Volcanion out - Pressure never falls by itself, only vents and overloads move it, and
Meltdown guarantees an overload eventually regardless of what you do.
