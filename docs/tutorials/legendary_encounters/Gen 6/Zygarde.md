## Zygarde — The Order Pokémon

Zygarde corrects whoever is winning - including itself. Pull far ahead and it claws you back;
let it run away with the fight and it will dismantle its own lead. The only losing move is
repeating yourself.

### BALANCE — invisible, but you can feel it

There's no number shown, only two signs: a line each turn saying who's ahead, and a callout when
Zygarde's guard visibly rises or falls. Guard rises the further ahead *you* pull; it falls the
further ahead *Zygarde* pulls. Every couple of turns Zygarde corrects whoever is leading -
resetting your stats or HP lead if it's you, undoing its own boosts or draining its own HP if
it's itself.

### Phase 0 — ORDER (100% → 50%)

Corrections are slow and only bite if you lead by a wide margin. A Zygarde Cell drifts in every
few turns - land a real hit on it that turn or the next, or Zygarde absorbs it and its guard
climbs a notch (up to five times). Lean on one strategy - boosting, healing, switching, or
setup - three turns running and Zygarde shuts that exact strategy off until you stop.

### Phase 1 — COMPLETE FORME (50% → 20%)

Zygarde transforms, correction gets strict (barely any lead is safe now), and Cells appear every
time the last one resolves. CORE ENFORCER charges over three turns and then slams whoever is
ahead - if that's Zygarde, it costs itself HP and one of its own absorbed Cells.

### Phase 2 — THOUSAND-YEAR BALANCE (20% → 10%)

LAND'S WRATH: the whole field resets, every restriction lifts, and Zygarde gives up everything it
has absorbed along with a chunk of its own guard. Play however you like from here - the meter
stops mattering.

### Phase 3 — SPENT (10%, the catch window)

Zygarde's power gives out entirely. Poké Balls open up now - "now is the moment."

### What actually beats it

Win steadily, not explosively - a wide lead invites a hard correction. Rotate your approach so no
single strategy runs three turns straight. Answer every Cell with real damage the turn after it
appears.

### What does not work

Racing to a huge lead fast. Turtling behind one repeated strategy. Panicking when Zygarde pulls
ahead - it corrects itself if you just keep playing.
