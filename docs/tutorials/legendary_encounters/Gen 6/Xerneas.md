## Xerneas — The Life Pokémon

Everything alive on the field feeds Xerneas's LIFE ENERGY - the more it has, the harder it is to
hurt. Win by making it spend that energy faster than the field can refill it.

### LIFE ENERGY — 0 to 10

The bar decides how much damage gets through. It rises: passively every turn, whenever a Fairy or
Grass move lands (either side!), when a LIFE BLOOM shatters, and when one of your Pokémon is at or
below 15% HP at the end of a turn. It falls: when you take 10%+ off Xerneas in a turn, and from the
mechanics below. A line calls out the band by name every time it changes, in both directions.

### Phase 0 — THE GIVING TREE (100% → 60%)

#### LIFE BLOOMS
Xerneas grows a LIFE BLOOM (up to 2 here). Each is a real screen effect - Mist, then Lucky Chant -
and can be broken with Defog, Brick Break, Psychic Fangs, Raging Bull, or Haze. Breaking one hands
its energy back to Xerneas, but regrowing costs more than that, so popping blooms is a slow win.

#### LIFE BLESSING
If your active Pokémon drops to 35% HP or below, Xerneas may bless it: heals it, cures its status,
raises its Defense and Sp. Def. If that Pokémon later faints, Xerneas gets a big chunk of life back -
so switch it out instead of trading it off. Switching lets the blessing lapse for nothing.

### Phase 1 — THE FOREST AWAKENS (60% → 30%)

Permanent Grassy Terrain goes up (heals your team too). A third bloom (Safeguard) becomes available -
it blocks status completely until popped, so it's worth breaking if you need to land a status move.

#### GEOMANCY
Telegraphed several turns ahead. If it resolves: Xerneas's Sp. Atk/Sp. Def/Speed spike, your stat
boosts are wiped, and a big life-scaled hit lands. Hit Xerneas hard during the charge-up to break it
early and waste its energy instead.

### Phase 2 — LIFE ETERNAL (30% → empty meter)

The meter flips: it's now a countdown draining every turn. Nothing on the field can faint, but only
for 5 turns at a time - the guard drops for 5 turns, then returns, cycling for as long as Phase 2
lasts. Get Xerneas below 25% and it forces itself back up by spending energy - so damage is never
wasted, it's the fastest way to drain the meter. Do NOT feed it here: every Fairy/Grass move and every
low-HP ally just extends the lock.

### Phase 3 — the catch window

When the meter empties, Xerneas gives everything it has left to your team (a big heal, one revive)
and falls silent, badly weakened. Now is the moment - throw the ball.

### What actually beats it

Poison and Steel types (they don't feed the meter). Avoiding Fairy/Grass moves entirely. Hitting
hard and often to force the mend-the-wound spend. Popping blooms when you need through a screen.
Switching a blessed Pokémon out instead of letting it die.

### What does not work

OHKOs, fixed-damage moves, Pain Split, Destiny Bond/Perish Song - all blocked outright. Setup and
evasion get wiped by Geomancy and the Phase 2 entry. Poké Balls do nothing until Phase 3.
