## Glastrier — The Wild Horse Pokémon

A slow, armored brute that gets colder and meaner the longer you keep attacking. Slow down
occasionally, or it becomes unkillable and unstoppable at the same time.

### FROST — 0 to 6, only ever climbs

Frost rises when you hit Glastrier, when it hits back, when either side uses an Ice or Water move,
and slowly on its own every couple of turns. Higher Frost means thicker armor and, eventually, a
progressive freeze on your Pokémon. The only way back down is landing a Fire-type hit.

### Phase 1 — Wild Horse (100% → 50%)

#### Icy armor
At Frost 2, 4, and 6, Glastrier's hide plates over and it shrugs off more damage each time. This
never reverses once it happens.

#### The chill sets in
Once Frost hits 4, your Pokémon's footing gives out (a Speed drop). Push Frost to 6 and it gets
trapped in place — it can't switch or flee.

#### Avalanche
Glastrier builds toward a slam every turn. Hitting it chips the buildup down; leaving it alone lets
Avalanche crash for a bigger hit the longer it's been building.

### Phase 2 — Permafrost (50% and below)

Frost locks at maximum, the armor is total, and Glastrier turns Unstoppable — flinches and status
moves stop working on it entirely, and it hits much harder. The freeze on your side is still
building — land a Fire-type move to crack the ice, strip Unstoppable, and buy yourself room to
breathe.

### Phase 3 — Frozen King (20% and below)

Every turn opens with Glastrier freezing a path across the field. Land two hits on it during that
turn to shatter the path; fail and Glacial Lance connects for a huge hit. Either way, the ice melts
a little. Poké Balls work once it's down to 10% HP.

### What actually beats it

Carry a Fire-type move. It's the only thing that ever brings Frost back down or turns off
Unstoppable in Permafrost. Pace your offense — don't dump everything into it at once, or the armor
and freeze escalate faster than you can handle.

### What does not work

Trying to flinch or status-lock Glastrier once it's Unstoppable — both are ignored outright.
Outlasting the freeze passively doesn't work either; only a Fire hit clears it.
