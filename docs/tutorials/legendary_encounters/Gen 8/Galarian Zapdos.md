## Galarian Zapdos — The Relentless Pursuer

Zapdos fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage.

> **Zapdos learns you every hit it lands, and forgets when you make it pay.**

### MOMENTUM — 0 to 5

Zapdos's every hit lands harder while Momentum is up.

Momentum rises when:

- **Zapdos lands a hit on you** (+1, +2 more on a real haymaker)
- **Zapdos knocks out your Pokémon** (+2)

Momentum falls when:

- **You land a big single hit** on Zapdos (18%+ of its HP in one shot, while Momentum is 3+) —
  **Stagger**, knocks it down 2 and shuts the combo damage off until it rebuilds
- **You hit Zapdos with Flying, Psychic or Fairy** — its own weakness (-1)
- **Zapdos attacks into your Protect** (-1)
- **Zapdos gets statused** (-1, for as long as the status holds)

At Momentum 2-4, every hit Zapdos lands is followed by extra chip damage — bigger the higher the
Momentum. At **Momentum 5**, it detonates into **RELENTLESS ASSAULT**: a genuine bonus hit at the
end of the turn, then burns back down to 3.

### PURSUIT

Switching out draws a free hit from Zapdos as your new Pokémon comes in — unless it's a **Ground-type**,
which shrugs the hit off entirely (and costs Zapdos a point of Momentum instead).

### Phase 1 — Opening (100% → 50%)

Everything above, running as described.

### Phase 2 — Breakneck Speed (50% → 15%)

> "Galarian Zapdos throws itself into a frenzy!"

Zapdos gets harder to dent and Pursuit hits harder. Momentum thresholds don't change.

### Phase 3 — Last Stand (≤15%, the catch window)

> "Zapdos refuses to stop! Now's the moment!"

Zapdos gets easier to dent and Poké Balls unblock. Relentless Assault now also costs Zapdos its own
HP — pushed hard enough, it can beat itself.

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Punish every hit that lands on you.** A Stagger (a real haymaker on Zapdos while Momentum is
   already up) is the single biggest swing in the fight.
2. **Lead with Flying, Psychic or Fairy moves** when you have them — every such hit chips Momentum
   even without a Stagger.
3. **Don't switch carelessly.** Bring a Ground-type if you expect to need to swap.
4. **Watch the combo-damage line.** It only shows up while Momentum is 2+ and unlocked — if it stops
   appearing, you broke the cycle.
5. **Statusing Zapdos costs it Momentum for as long as the status holds** — a real answer, not just
   a stall.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| OHKO moves, Endeavor, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Switching in anything but a Ground-type to dodge Pursuit | Pursuit still lands |
| Throwing a ball before 15% HP | Blocked until then |
