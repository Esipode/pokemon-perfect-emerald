## Eternatus — The Gigantic Pokémon

Eternatus fights behind the usual legendary package: a 90% damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is
guaranteed no matter how the fight goes.

> **Energy never stops climbing.**

One meter, 0 to 100. Every mechanic either feeds it or bleeds it, and passing turns alone push it
up — there is no safe way to stall.

---

### ENERGY — 0 to 100

```
  0–24   quiet
 25–49   Energy distortion ripples outward
 50–74   the energy grows unstable        (Eternatus's crit chance rises)
 75–100  DYNAMAX SURGE                    (Nodes and the Cannon appear far more often)
```

Above 80, a warning keeps repeating every turn — that is not a new threat, just the existing one
getting louder.

```
  +1   every turn, unconditionally
  +3   Eternatus attacks
 +15   a Node detonates unchallenged
 +20   the Dynamax Cannon fires unchallenged
 -20   you interrupt a charging Cannon
 -25   you land a hit during a Core window
```

---

### Nodes — deny them

An energy Node can form at any point in the fight and lingers for 3 turns. Leave it alone and it
detonates: Energy jumps and your side takes a real hit. **Hit Eternatus while a Node is up and it
fizzles for nothing** — no Energy, no damage.

### The Dynamax Cannon — interrupt it

From 60% HP on, Eternatus periodically announces it's aiming a Dynamax Cannon and spends two turns
charging. Let it finish and it's a heavy hit plus a big Energy jump. **Hit Eternatus during the
charge and the shot breaks apart** — no damage, and Energy actually drops.

### Core windows — punish them

Only in the final phase, Eternatus's core exposes itself for two turns. Its guard drops hard while
it's open. **Any hit landed during a Core window closes it early and cuts Energy directly** — the
single best thing you can do to the meter all fight.

---

### Phase 0 — THE ALIEN CORE (100% → 60%)

Just the Node cycle. Get comfortable denying them.

### Phase 1 — ETERNAMAX (60% → 30%)

Eternatus transforms, its Special Attack rises, and the Dynamax Cannon comes online. The Node cycle
keeps running underneath it.

### Phase 2 — THE DARKEST DAY (30% → the end)

Energy is forced to maximum immediately, and your active Pokémon gets a one-time Dynamax
Resonance — +2 Attack, free. Nodes and the Cannon both run faster and more often. Core windows
start opening periodically — that's the counterplay this phase is built around.

**At 10% HP**, Eternatus falters and the catch window opens: Poké Balls are unblocked at a real
catch rate, and it can't be knocked out while you're throwing.

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Always be ready to punish, not just attack.** A hit that lands during a Node/Cannon/Core
   window is worth more than the same hit landing on a quiet turn.
2. **Watch for the Cannon telegraph** and spend the charge turns interrupting it, not attacking
   freely — the payout for ignoring it is large.
3. **In Phase 2, prioritize any hit during a Core window** — it's the only thing that cuts Energy
   directly instead of just riding it up.
4. **Don't stall.** Energy climbs every turn regardless of what you do.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| OHKO moves, Endeavor, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Toxic stall | The damage never ramps |
| Waiting out the fight passively | Energy still climbs every turn either way |
