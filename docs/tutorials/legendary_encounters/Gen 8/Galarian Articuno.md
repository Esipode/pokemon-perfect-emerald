## Galarian Articuno — The Cruel Pokémon

Articuno fights behind the usual legendary package: a damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the catch window opens.

> **Articuno reads your patterns, not your exact moves.**

It only ever sees your move's **category** — Physical, Special, or Status. Varying category is how
you stay unread.

---

### FOCUS — 0 to 3

Articuno's tell. At **3**, it fires **PSYCHIC LOCK** — one random disruption — and resets to 0.

Focus rises when you:

- **Repeat a category** from your last move
- **Hit Articuno super-effectively** (Dark, Ghost, Ice, Rock or Electric)
- **Use a status move** — always, whether or not it's a repeat
- **Switch**

### PSYCHIC LOCK

One of, at random:

- Your last move used is **sealed** for 3 turns
- You are **trapped** — no switching, no fleeing
- Your held item is **shorted out** for 3 turns
- Your Attack or Sp. Atk is **cut hard**

---

### PREDICT — every turn

Articuno guesses your next category. Guess right and it fires a **counter-blast** straight to your
Pokémon's HP — a direct write, so Substitute doesn't help. Guess wrong (you varied) and it **loses a
point of Focus.**

```
16%  Phase 0
24%  Phase 1 and 2
```

There's no message telling you which way it guessed — only the outcome. Keep varying category and
you starve both Focus **and** the counter-blast at once.

### MIND GAMES — every 4 turns

Articuno offers a choice:

| Answer | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **RESIST** | Your next hit is a guaranteed crit — but Articuno's Focus rises |
| **YIELD** | Articuno's Attack rises, but its Focus falls |

---

### Phase 0 — THE MIND'S EYE (100% → 50%)

Focus, Predict, and Mind Games are all live as described above.

### Phase 1 — PERFECT PREDICTION (50% → 20%)

> "Its eyes flare with psychic energy!"

Nothing else changes — the counter-blast just gets meaner, 16% → 24%.

### Phase 2 — MIND BREAK (20% → 10%)

> "Your own mind begins to unravel!"

Every 3 turns, Articuno fires a **Psychic Lock**-style disruption **automatically**, Focus be
damned. Everything from Phase 0 stacks on top — Focus, Predict, and Mind Games all keep running.

### Phase 3 — the catch window (≤10%)

Its focus shatters. Everything the fight raised comes off, Articuno drops to a token sliver of HP,
and Poké Balls unblock. This is the only point the fight is meant to be caught.

---

### What actually beats it

1. **Never use the same category twice in a row.** That alone kills the Focus feed and the
   counter-blast together.
2. **Don't lean on status moves.** They feed Focus every time, repeat or not.
3. **Watch what lands on you at the end of a turn you varied.** A hit means it guessed right anyway
   — just outlast it, the odds are the same next turn.
4. **Stay in.** Switching feeds Focus for free.
5. **In Phase 2, expect a forced disruption every 3 turns** no matter how careful you've been —
   plan around it, don't try to prevent it.

### What does not work

| The plan | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| OHKO moves, Endeavor, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Perish Song, Destiny Bond | All blocked outright |
| A 4x weakness | Effectiveness is capped at 2x |
| Substitute stalling the counter-blast | It's a direct HP write |
| Throwing a ball early | Blocked until the catch window |
