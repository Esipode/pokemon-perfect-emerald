## Spectrier — The Swift Horse Pokémon

A shadow that learns your fear. The longer you play it safe, the harder it gets to hit — and the
worse things get from there.

### DREAD — the meter that never lets go

Dread climbs on its own, a little every few turns, and jumps whenever you:
- let a Pokémon faint,
- switch out twice or more in a stretch,
- use a status move,
- or get hit by one of Spectrier's Ghost-type attacks.

The higher Dread climbs, the more evasive Spectrier gets, the more Fear Mark bites, and the closer
the fight gets to its final phase — Dread maxing out forces that phase early, HP threshold or not.

### Phase 1 — Swift Horse (100% → 50%)

#### Fear Mark
Periodically traps your active Pokémon and sharpens Spectrier's Special Attack. It doesn't let go
on its own — land real damage on Spectrier to break it.

#### Shadow Trail
Once Dread is high enough, a ghostly trail starts building behind Spectrier, stacking its evasion
higher over time. A Dark-type move that connects burns off one stack — and feeds Spectrier's
Grudge instead.

#### Shadow Charge
Spectrier telegraphs a gathering shadow one turn before it lands. Answer with a Dark-type move to
ground it for a small hit; anything else eats the full blow.

#### Dread lock
At high Dread, Spectrier occasionally locks your last-used move out of your hands for a few turns.

### Phase 2 — Soul Steed (50% → 25%)

Faster, hungrier. Shadow Charge comes around more often, and the Grudge you built purging Shadow
Trail now makes each Charge hit harder.

### Phase 3 — Nightmare Rider (the catch window)

Triggered at 25% HP or the moment Dread maxes out. Spectrier vanishes into a buildup turn — no
attack, just a warning — then unleashes one unavoidable Astral Barrage. After that its guard drops
completely. Once it's down to 10% HP, Poké Balls work.

### What actually beats it

Keep a Dark-type move in your lineup — it grounds Shadow Charge and purges Shadow Trail in one
swing. Attack, don't stall: passive play and repeated switching are exactly what feeds Dread.

### What does not work

Turtling behind switches or status moves only makes Spectrier harder to hit. There's no way to stop
the Nightmare phase from arriving eventually — only to arrive at it with Dread as low as possible.
