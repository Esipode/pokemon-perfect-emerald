## Regieleki — The Electrified Pokémon
It never stops moving, and every turn it isn't hit, it gets faster.

### CHARGE — 0 to 10
Rises from: Regieleki attacking, taking an Electric hit, you switching, a turn passing, Electric
Terrain, and Regieleki simply being faster than you that turn.
Falls only from: you landing a damaging hit on it. That's the one brake.

### Phase 1 — Online (100% → 50%)
#### Overcharge
At CHARGE 5 it speeds up and hits harder but grows frailer. At CHARGE 8 it pushes further in the
same direction. Drop CHARGE below 5 and it settles back down.
#### Flash Step
At its highest tier, it has a chance each turn to strike early, hurting you and pushing its own
CHARGE even higher. The longer you let it sit at max tier, the more this happens.

### Phase 2 — Grid Expansion (50% → 15%)
Its own attacks now feed CHARGE twice as fast. The instant this phase begins, a one-turn window
opens: land a solid hit or a Ground-type move that turn and the surge fizzles or grounds out.
Do nothing useful, and it detonates for heavy damage.

### Phase 3 — Critical Overload (the catch window)
At 15% HP it locks in at max speed and power and unloads Electro Drift, then collapses -
exhausted, guard down. Poké Balls work once it drops to 10% HP.

### What actually beats it
Keep landing hits — that's the only thing that ever brings CHARGE down. A Ground-type move is
your answer to the Power Surge specifically. Don't let it sit at max Overcharge; every turn there
is a coin flip for Flash Step.

### What does not work
Status moves, stalling, and switching all just feed CHARGE. There's no safe way to wait this
fight out.
