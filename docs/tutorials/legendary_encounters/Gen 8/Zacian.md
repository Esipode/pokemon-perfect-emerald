## Zacian — The Warrior Pokemon
Every clean hit Zacian lands on you teaches it something. Watch how it fights, and it tells you
exactly what's coming next.

### TECHNIQUE — 0 to 100
Every hit Zacian lands raises Technique. The band it's sitting in decides its Stance, so the meter
climbs no matter what you do — Protect is the only thing that pushes it back down.

### Phase 0 — Every Stance (100% → 50%)
#### Read the Stance
- **SWIFT** (low Technique): faster. Outrun it or take the first hit.
- **POWER**: hits harder. Brace for it.
- **PRECISION**: sharper aim and crits. Respect the accuracy.
- **EXECUTION** (high Technique): no stat boost — it's just landed everything it's tried.
#### Sword Marks and the Chain
Landed hits stack a Mark on you. Three stacks and Zacian cashes them in for bonus damage, then
resets. Landed hits also build a Chain — three in a row arms Zacian's next strike as unstoppable
(a warning prints first). Protect strips both Marks and the Chain, but costs Zacian's Technique —
don't lean on it every turn, and don't Protect back-to-back: two predicted defensive turns in a
row and Zacian reads it and gets sharper for free.

### Phase 1 — Crowned Sword (50% → 15%)
Technique now climbs on its own, so Stances cycle faster no matter what. Sword Arts come online —
Rising Slash, Cross Slash, Meteor Slash, and Royal Cut rotate in on a cooldown, each with its own
tell and payload.

### Phase 2 — Master Swordsman (15% → 0%, the catch window at ≤10%)
Zacian locks into EXECUTION and a Sword Art fires with every hit it lands — this is the gauntlet.
Once its Technique is maxed, it charges the BEHEMOTH BLADE: a single devastating strike, after
which its light fades and the sword's edge is spent for good. Poke Balls unlock once it drops to
10% HP.

### What actually beats it
Stay clean — every hit you take teaches it, so minimize the free ones. Save Protect for when the
Chain warning fires, not as a reflex, and never two turns running.

### What does not work
Protect-stalling — it costs you Technique discipline and Zacian starts predicting it. Switching
clears Marks off your old mon for free, but does nothing to slow Zacian's own Stance or Sword Art
progression.
