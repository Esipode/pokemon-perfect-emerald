## Enamorus — The Love-Hate Pokémon

Every Pokémon you send out can fall under Enamorus's spell. Let it happen and your mon gets tougher
but less reliable. Fight through it and you can turn the charm against Enamorus itself.

### AFFECTION — Neutral → Fond → Enamored → Obsessed → Heartbreak

Enamorus's hits push your Pokémon up this ladder. Enamored and Obsessed grant a real Defense boost
but risk your move fizzling that turn, and once Enamorus is bonded to your whole team, being hit
while Enamored bleeds pain onto a random teammate too. Hitting Enamorus back while Enamored or
Obsessed shatters the charm into **Heartbreak** instead: a few turns of immunity to being re-charmed,
a solid hit on Enamorus, and a visible crack in its guard.

### Phase 0 — Incarnate (100% → 50%)

#### Charm
Every Enamorus hit that lands advances your Pokémon's Affection one step.

#### Hesitation
While Enamored or Obsessed, your Pokémon has a real chance to freeze up and skip its next move.

#### Jealousy
Switch out an Enamored+ Pokémon and whoever you send in inherits a Fond starting point.

#### Heartbreak
Attack Enamorus while your Pokémon is Enamored or Obsessed to break the charm outright — it costs
your stat bonus, but it hurts Enamorus and visibly shakes its guard for a few turns.

### Phase 1 — Wrath of Love (50% → 20%)

Enamorus shifts to its Therian Forme, gains a new move, and reaching Obsessed becomes possible. Its
bond with your team also awakens: from here on, every landed hit also echoes some pain onto a
teammate on your bench.

### Phase 2 — Heartstorm (20% → 10%)

Emotions spiral without warning. Every turn, whether or not Enamorus even attacks, someone's
Affection can climb or shatter into Heartbreak on its own.

### Phase 3 — Spring's Embrace (the catch window)

At 10% HP, Enamorus pours everything still lingering into one Fairy-type surge — the more of your
team it had charmed, the harder it hits. Once the dust settles, every emotional effect clears and
its guard drops for good. That's your window to throw the ball.

### What actually beats it

- Push through Charm rather than avoiding it — Heartbreak only exists on the other side of being
  charmed, and it's your best tool against Enamorus's guard.
- Watch for the guard-changing lines; they mean Enamorus is easier to hurt right now.
- A fresh switch-in starts clean unless you're pulling out someone Enamored — time your swaps.

### What does not work

- Poké Balls are blocked until the very end, however low its HP gets before then.
- Recharming an already-Heartbroken Pokémon doesn't work — it's immune until the effect fades on
  its own.
