## Zamazenta — The Warrior Pokemon
Zamazenta grows tougher with every blow it survives. Hit it small and safe, and it gets sturdier
but the fight drags. Hit it hard, and you crack that sturdiness open — but it remembers, and pays
you back.

### RESOLVE and SHIELD LAYERS
Resolve climbs as the fight goes on. At 30/60/90 Resolve, Zamazenta grows a Shield Layer (each one
makes it noticeably tankier). Landing a big chunk of damage in a single turn cracks the newest
layer back off — but banks toward GUARD COUNTER. Once enough is banked, Zamazenta's next attack
that lands hits back harder. Letting a crack happen is a trade, not a mistake.

### Phase 0 — The Fortress Builds (100% → 50%)
#### Iron Head rebuilds a layer
If Zamazenta lands Iron Head on you, it patches a Shield Layer back up. Don't let it sit and tank.

### Phase 1 — Crowned Shield (≤50%)
Zamazenta stops budging: every turn it clears its own lowered stats and cures its own status.
Debuffing or statusing it only buys one turn.
#### Break the Fortress
Land three physical hits, three special hits, and three status effects on it (each category
tracked separately) to bring down LEFT, CENTER, and RIGHT. Get all three down and the Fortress
collapses, leaving it cracked open for good. A one-note attacker never finishes this — mix it up.

### Phase 2 — Last Stand (≤20%)
Zamazenta digs in: it can't be knocked out this phase, and its defenses got one more boost. Poke
Balls are still blocked — this isn't the moment yet.

### Phase 3 — Behemoth Bash (≤10%, the catch window)
Zamazenta unloads everything it's been holding in one last hit, scaled by how much Guard Counter
charge it was carrying. After that, every meter resets, its guard drops for good, and its Poke
Ball is finally there for the throwing.

### What actually beats it
Steady, varied pressure. Mix move categories once it's Crowned, don't feed it a huge alpha strike
into a fresh Shield Layer, and be ready to eat one big counter if you do swing hard.

### What does not work
Stalling it out with status or stat drops past Phase 1 — Fortress Cleanse resets both every turn.
Trying to catch it before ≤10% HP — the Ball just bounces.
