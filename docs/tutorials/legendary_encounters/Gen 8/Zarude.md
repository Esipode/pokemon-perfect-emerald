## Zarude — The Rogue Monkey Pokémon

The jungle grows against you the longer you fight carelessly. Zarude gets stronger, tankier and
more dangerous the deeper it gets, and only your own play can hold it back.

### TERRITORY — 0 to 6, sets the jungle's growth tier

Landing Grass moves, hurting Zarude, or letting vines expire all push Territory up. Anticipating
Zarude's ambush is the only way to pull it back down. Each tier crossed (Overgrown at 2, Jungle at
4) hardens Zarude a little and heals it — the jungle is rewarding your recklessness.

### Phase 0 — Territorial Fight (100% → 50%)

#### Vines
Once Territory hits 2, vines periodically bind your Pokémon for a few turns, slowing it and biting
away a little HP each turn — until they wither, at which point Territory climbs again.

#### Jungle Ambush
Once Territory hits 4, Zarude starts telegraphing an ambush a few turns out. Protect on the
telegraphed turn and the ambush backfires, knocking Territory back down. Ignore it and it hits
hard.

### Phase 1 — Jungle Sovereign (50% → 20%)

Zarude beats its chest, forces the jungle to Deep Jungle, and raises a Jungle Barrier — three
shield charges standing in for its troop. Deal real damage in a turn and you break a charge. Let a
turn pass without breaking one and the troop rallies, boosting Zarude's Attack.

### Phase 2 — Guardian of the Jungle (≤20%, catch window at ≤10%)

The jungle forces Ancient Jungle and Jungle Fury hits Zarude's Attack again. Every so often Zarude
tries to feed off the jungle and heal — hit hard that same turn and the feeding is cut short. Watch
for one final Jungle Rage burst once Zarude is nearly out of HP. Poké Balls open up at 10% HP.

### What actually beats it

- Keep the pressure up: a real hit most turns keeps Territory from climbing and breaks the barrier
  before it can feed Zarude's Rage.
- Protect through the telegraphed ambush — it's the only way to bring Territory back down once
  vines and tiers have it climbing.
- Don't let a turn go quiet in Phase 1 or 2 — an unbroken barrier charge or an unanswered feed
  attempt both make the fight longer.

### What does not work

- Stalling out the vine bind by switching — it traps you in place.
- Poké Balls before 10% HP — they're blocked outright until then.
