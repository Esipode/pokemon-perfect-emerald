# Regidrago — The Dragon Orb Pokemon

An ancient, half-awake dragon running on stored power instead of its own strength. Every hit that
lands, every Dragon move thrown, every Pokemon that faints — it all feeds the same meter, and once
that meter is full, Regidrago stops holding back.

### DRAGON ENERGY — 0 to 100

Climbs from real damage traded (either direction, bigger hits feed it more), from Dragon-type
moves landing, and from your Pokemon fainting. Drifts up slowly on its own too. At 25/50/75/100 it
permanently powers up a little more. Letting several turns pass with no damage at all traded turns
this into Hunger — worse for you than just feeding it.

### Awakened (100% → 50% HP)

#### Dragon Orbs
An orb periodically forms near Regidrago. Hit it hard within a few turns or it gets devoured — a
big free chunk of Energy. Destroy it first (a solid hit while it's up) and you deny that feed.

#### Hunger
Go three turns straight without any damage trading — no hits landing either way — and Regidrago
gets hungry: it traps you in place and buffs its own Attack and Speed. Trade a hit to snap it out
of it.

#### Dragon Maw
Its jaws start glowing — a telegraphed haymaker a couple of turns out. Hit hard while it's charging
and you break the charge. Ignore it and it lands a big hit and banks Energy.

### Ancient Dragon (50% → 20% HP)

Energy now climbs on its own every turn regardless of what happens in battle — you're on a clock.
Energy Theft locks onto your active Pokemon and chips it every turn until you destroy an Orb, which
breaks the lock.

### Dragon Overload (20% HP and below — the catch window)

Energy is forced to maximum, its power spikes one last time, and then Energy drains on its own
every turn instead of rising. When it bottoms out — or Regidrago is chipped to 10% HP first — it
fires Eternabeam, a finisher scaled by how much Energy it had banked, and its guard finally drops.
Balls only work from here on.

### What actually beats it

- Trade real damage every turn — it starves Hunger and keeps the meter from being the only thing
  moving.
- Destroy Dragon Orbs on sight; it's the only way to cut Theft's lock and deny a big Energy feed.
- Watch for the Maw telegraph and swing hard the turn it's up.

### What does not work

- Stalling. Passing turns without trading damage triggers Hunger, and Ancient Dragon's auto-rise
  ignores stalling entirely.
- Throwing Balls before Eternabeam — they're blocked until then no matter the HP.
