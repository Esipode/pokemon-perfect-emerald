## Calyrex — The King Pokémon

### Location: Route 134 - After 30+ legendaries caught

Calyrex judges you by decrees. Break them and its Authority grows, hardening its guard and stacking the last gamble against you.

### AUTHORITY — 0 to 10
- **Rises:** breaking a Royal Decree (+1), losing your lead during a King's Command (+2), failing the King's Gambit (+1).
- **Falls:** finishing a King's Command (-2), winning the King's Gambit (-3).
- At 3, 6 and 9 the field changes (psychic terrain, then gravity, then a harder guard). From 6 up, it keeps reminding you.

### Phase 0 — ROYAL DECREES (100% → 50%)
#### Decree
Each decree lasts 3 turns and they alternate.
- **Do not switch:** any switch during the decree breaks it.
- **Do not stay weakened:** ending two turns in a row with your lead at or below 33% HP breaks it.

### Phase 1 — THE RIDER RETURNS (50% → 15%)
Calyrex mounts an Ice or Shadow steed (random) and gains a signature move.
#### King's Command
Keep your lead standing through 3 turns. Success lowers Authority. If your lead faints, Authority rises and Calyrex's Attack goes up.

### Phase 2 — KINGDOM RESTORED (15% and below)
Its guard is at its highest. Two turns later comes the **King's Gambit**: the lower its Authority, the likelier it falters. Fail and the steed's finisher hits your lead hard, then Calyrex must recharge.

### Catch window (10% and below)
Calyrex's guard breaks and Poké Balls unlock. This opens from any phase.

### What actually beats it
- Obey each decree and switch only between decrees.
- Survive King's Commands.
- A clean run makes the Gambit near-certain to fail.

### What does not work
- One-hit KOs, fixed damage, HP swaps and shared-KO moves.
- Poké Balls before 10% HP.
- Sacrificing a Pokémon to skip a Command.
