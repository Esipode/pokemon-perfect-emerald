## Jirachi — The Wish Pokémon

Jirachi fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be killed** until it has spent its last wish.

None of that is the fight. The fight is that **Jirachi is a mirror.** It does not react to what you
did to it — it reacts to *how you fight*, and then wishes for the same thing.

### The core loop

```
Jirachi gathers WISH ENERGY every turn.  At full energy it makes a wish.

WHAT it wishes for is decided by your HEART - a hidden read of your play,
moved by every move you use.  Attack a lot and it wishes for POWER.  Use
status moves and it wishes for PEACE.  Play in between and it wishes for
WONDER.

Every wish costs it something - and that cost is your opening.

Then it asks you:  "Will you make a wish too?"
  YES -> you get the same boon it just took, and its wish costs it NOTHING.
         It also wishes again sooner.
  NO  -> you get nothing, and it pays the full price.

It gets exactly THREE wishes.  The third is the MIRACLE, and your Heart
picks that one too.  Survive the Miracle and its tags go dark.
```

### Reading the tags

Jirachi never tells you the numbers. It tells you the colour of its tags, and the line changes the
moment the read changes:

| The line | What it means | The wish it will make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Jirachi's tags glow a fierce red.* | You have been attacking | **Wish of Power** |
| *Jirachi's tags shimmer, silver and unsettled.* | You have been mixing it up | **Wish of Wonder** |
| *Jirachi's tags glow a still, deep blue.* | You have been using status moves | **Wish of Peace** |

Every damaging move you use pushes the meter toward red; every status move pushes it toward blue.
A move that **missed still counts** — Jirachi reads intent, not results. A multi-hit move counts once.
You start in the middle, in silver.

The clock is just as loud:

> *Jirachi's tags begin to chime softly…* → two turns of energy
> *Jirachi's tags are ringing!* → it wishes at the top of the next turn

### The three wishes

Each wish applies Jirachi's own boon first, then asks whether you want the same thing. **The right
answer is different every time** — that is the whole point of the prompt.

#### Wish of Power *(red)*

> *Jirachi wished for overwhelming power!*

It takes **+2 Attack and +2 Sp. Attack**.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **YES** | You get **+2 Attack and +2 Sp. Attack** too — but its bulk is untouched |
| **NO** | Its Defense and Sp. Defense drop **one stage each**, *and* its damage reduction falls a rung **permanently** |

Refusing Power is the aggressive line, and the only way to permanently soften it.

#### Wish of Peace *(blue)*

> *Jirachi wished for safety.*

It takes **Reflect, Light Screen and Safeguard** for 5 turns — and its damage reduction climbs a rung
**whether or not you wish alongside it**.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **YES** | You get **Reflect, Light Screen and Safeguard** on your side too |
| **NO** | *Jirachi drifts, lost inside its own wish…* — it **loses this turn's action** |

Refusing usually wins you more, unless you are about to be status-locked and want the Safeguard.

#### Wish of Wonder *(silver)*

> *Jirachi wished for something it could not name…*

A one-in-four roll:

| Roll | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| *Jirachi wished for renewed strength!* | It heals **20%** |
| *Jirachi wished upon a falling star!* | **Tailwind** for 4 turns, and a star hits you for **12%** |
| *Jirachi wished for stillness.* | **Every stat stage on the field resets** — yours *and* its own |
| *Jirachi wished to play!* | **You** heal 25%. It gains nothing at all |

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **YES** | You heal **25%** |
| **NO** | It **loses this turn's action** |

### The hidden price of saying yes

Every YES leaves Jirachi with energy still in the bank, so the next wish arrives **a turn sooner**.
Accept all three and you reach the Miracle noticeably early — against a Jirachi that never paid for
any of them.

### The tags blaze

The moment Jirachi drops to **40% HP**:

> *Jirachi's tags blaze with gathered light!*

Its energy fills instantly. Whatever wish is next arrives within a turn. A hurt Jirachi wishes harder.

### The Miracle — the third wish

> *JIRACHI'S FINAL WISH!*

**No prompt this time.** Your Heart picks it, and you live with it.

| Your Heart | The Miracle | What you are fighting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Red** | *Jirachi wished for overwhelming power - and the wish was granted!* | **A race.** +50% Attack and Sp. Attack, but its damage reduction reduces to 80%. It can end you in two moves; you can end it in three |
| **Blue** | *Jirachi wished to live - and the wish was granted!* | **A wall.** It heals 20%, raises **permanent** Reflect, Light Screen and Safeguard, and its reduction climbs to 94%. Long, not impossible — cash in the setup you have been building |
| **Silver** | *Jirachi wished upon every star at once!* | **A clock.** Permanent Tailwind so it moves first, its reduction at 80% — the softest of the three — and **a star falls on you for 10% every single turn**. Finish it or lose |

While a Miracle is running you will hear *"Jirachi's miracle is still burning."* every turn.

### Catching it

Poké Balls are **blocked for the entire fight**. Jirachi also cannot be killed before the Miracle
plays, however hard you hit it — if you have already earned the kill, it will tell you:

> *Jirachi holds on - its last wish is not yet spent.*

Survive the Miracle and grind it to **10% HP**:

> *Jirachi's tags have gone dark. Its wishes are spent.*
> *It's worn out - now is the moment to catch it!*

Everything comes off at once — the damage reduction, the immunities, the type-effectiveness cap,
the screens, the star clock — and Poké Balls are unlocked at a generous catch rate. From there it is
an ordinary Pokémon battle.

### What it fights with

Steel/Psychic with **Serene Grace**, which doubles every secondary effect in the set.

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Doom Desire** | Its signature, and the whole encounter in one move: a wish that arrives two turns later. A second clock to track |
| **Meteor Mash** | STAB physical — and Serene Grace makes its Attack raise a **40%** chance, so it snowballs on its own |
| **Psychic** | STAB special, with a **20%** Sp. Defense drop |
| **Thunderbolt** | Coverage into the Water and Flying types that would otherwise wall Steel/Psychic, with **20%** paralysis |

It runs the smart-trainer AI.

### Anti-cheese

- **Putting it to sleep is a trap, not a lock.** Jirachi is the Pokémon that sleeps for a thousand
  years — sleep makes it **dream**, and dreams are where wishes come from. An asleep Jirachi gains
  **double** Wish Energy: *"Jirachi sleeps… and its dreams grow stronger."* You have just put the
  Miracle on a two-turn clock. Wake it up, or race it deliberately.
- **Toxic does nothing.** It is part Steel and cannot be poisoned at all.
- **Stalling makes it worse.** Wish Energy is a plain turn counter, so Protect and recovery stalling
  only *accelerate* the fight into the Miracle.
- **Setting up six stat stages is not safe.** One Wonder outcome wipes every stat stage on the
  field, and the Miracle of Life is aimed squarely at the player who got there by stalling.
- **OHKO moves, Endeavor, Super Fang, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Destiny Bond and Perish Song** are
  all shut out — they would otherwise sidestep the survive clamp and skip the whole fight.
- **Taunt, Encore and Disable** are harmless. It is a wild Pokémon with four attacking moves and no
  status moves to shut off.
