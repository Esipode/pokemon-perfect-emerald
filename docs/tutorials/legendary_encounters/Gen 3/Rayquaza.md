## Rayquaza — The Sky Guardian

Rayquaza fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage.

None of that is the fight. Every legendary before it is a fight over a **number**. Rayquaza is a
fight over a **position**.

### The core loop

```
RAYQUAZA CLIMBS.  The higher it gets, the less your attacks are worth,
and the faster ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE builds.  Pressure only builds while
it is AIRBORNE.  When the Pressure fills, it vanishes into the
stratosphere - and the turn after, SKYFALL lands on you for a third of
your health.

You cannot out-damage it in the sky.  You have to BRING IT DOWN.

Two things pull it down:
  1. DISTURB THE ATMOSPHERE.  Put any weather on the field and Rayquaza
     must descend to tear it away - but it descends ANGRY, and the
     anger stacks.
  2. SHOOT IT DOWN.  Land enough damage in a single turn and it falls
     out of the sky on its own.

Grounded Rayquaza is hittable, and its Pressure clock is STOPPED.
That is the entire game: you are buying grounded turns, and paying for
them either in tempo or in how hard it hits you afterwards.
```

### Reading the altitude

Rayquaza never tells you a number. It tells you where it is, and it tells you **every single time
that changes**:

| The line | Where it is | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Rayquaza is dragged down to the field - it's within reach!* | **Grounded** | Softest guard in the phase. The Pressure clock is stopped |
| *Rayquaza lifts away on a rising current.* | **Low** | Guard up a little. Pressure starts building |
| *Rayquaza climbs higher - it's hard to even see!* | **High** | Guard up further, and it starts reading your attacks |
| *Rayquaza has disappeared into the heavens!* | **Stratospheric** | Nothing reaches it this turn - but it loses its turn too |

While it is High, a line repeats every turn to remind you the clock is running:

> *Rayquaza circles far beyond your reach.*

It climbs one rung on a timer, and **it never climbs past High on its own**. The stratosphere only
ever means Skyfall.

### The Pressure clock

Pressure builds only while Rayquaza is off the ground, and nothing but Skyfall spends it. The
warnings re-fire on every real change, and the whole build-up re-announces after each Skyfall:

> *The air around Rayquaza is growing heavy.* → about halfway
> *The sky is screaming. Something is about to give!* → Skyfall is close

Then:

> *The atmosphere itself buckles!*
> *Rayquaza has disappeared into the heavens!*

That turn is a **mutual dead turn** — nothing you do reaches it, and it does not attack either. The
turn after:

> *Something massive is coming down…*
> *RAYQUAZA FALLS OUT OF THE SKY!*

Skyfall hits for **30% of your max HP** (40% once it Megas, 33% in the final phase), and Rayquaza is
**not** protected on the way down, so its move and yours both land on top of it. It is the hardest
turn in the fight.

It is also the best one. Rayquaza lands **grounded, with its clock at zero.** The Skyfall is how you
get your turn — if you can survive it, that is your damage window.

### The two levers

#### 1. Disturb the atmosphere

Rayquaza has **Air Lock**, so weather does nothing while it is out. Set weather anyway. It has to
come down and rip the sky apart:

> *Rayquaza dives to tear the weather apart!*
> *Rayquaza has had enough!*

You get it on the ground and the clock stops — and it takes **+1 Attack and +1 Sp. Attack**. That
price **stacks**. Vent four times and you are fighting a +4 Rayquaza on the ground.

Bring your own **Kyogre or Groudon** and it answers personally, for double the anger:

> *The sky answers to Rayquaza alone!*

Weather set while Rayquaza is already grounded is not wasted — it simply sits there and pulls it
back down the moment it lifts off.

#### 2. Shoot it down

Land roughly **6% of its max HP in a single turn** while it is airborne and it falls:

> *That blow knocked Rayquaza out of the air!*

This is harder the higher it is, because the guard eats more of your damage — a strong
super-effective hit will usually drag it out of Low, but only a genuinely huge one reaches it at
High. Chip, multi-hits and status damage all count toward the same total.

This lever works in **every phase**, and it is the answer for a party carrying no weather at all.

### High Rayquaza reads you

At High altitude Rayquaza predicts your strongest option and slips it:

> *Rayquaza reads your intent and rolls away on the wind!*

**Special attacks always reach it. Physical ones can be read.** If your strongest option is physical,
expect to lose turns up there — which is another reason not to leave it in the sky.

### The phases

| HP | Phase | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100–50% | **The Sky Guardian** | Climbs every 3rd turn. Both levers available |
| < 50% | **DELTA ASCENSION** | Mega Evolves. Climbs **every turn**, Pressure builds twice as fast at High, Skyfall hits for 40% |
| < 22% | **The atmosphere breaks** | The guard collapses. Weather becomes real — and hostile to Rayquaza too |
| ≤ 10% | **Weakened** | Everything comes off. Poké Balls unlock |

#### 50% — Delta Ascension

> *Rayquaza absorbs the energy surrounding the battlefield!*
> **DELTA ASCENSION**

It Mega Evolves, gains **Dragon Ascent**, and **Delta Stream** puts up mysterious strong winds.

That is not flavour — it is the fight taking a tool away. Strong winds are *primal* weather and
**cannot be overwritten**, so lever 1 is dead for the rest of the phase. Shooting it down is now
your only way to bring it back to earth.

The winds also neutralise Flying's own weaknesses. The type-effectiveness cap had already clamped
the 4x Ice weakness to 2x; strong winds take it the rest of the way to **neutral**. Ice-stacking a
Mega Rayquaza stops working.

#### 22% — The atmosphere breaks

> *Rayquaza's hold on the sky is coming apart!*

The strong winds collapse. Its guard drops hard — this is roughly a **3x** swing in the damage you
deal. In exchange, the sky goes chaotic: every other turn it throws out rain, sun, sandstorm, hail
or clear skies at random, overwriting anything you set.

Air Lock is long gone by now, so this is the **only phase where weather is real** — and Rayquaza is
Dragon/Flying with no sand or hail immunity. The atmosphere finally hurts it too.

It is also kept alive here, so an oversized hit cannot skip the catch window.

#### 10% — the catch window

> *Rayquaza's ascent gives out. It cannot stay aloft.*
> *Rayquaza has fallen back to its true shape, too spent to rise again.*
> *It's worn out - now is the moment to catch it!*

It reverts to base Rayquaza, the whole machine goes quiet, every encounter rule comes off, and Poké
Balls unlock. From there it is an ordinary Pokémon battle.

### What it fights with

Dragon/Flying with **Air Lock** (**Delta Stream** once it Megas).

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Dragon Claw** | Reliable physical STAB |
| **Extreme Speed** | +2 priority — it gets a hit in on the turns you were counting on being faster |
| **Hurricane** | Special STAB with confusion. Its own Air Lock suppresses the rain-perfect accuracy, which is the correct and slightly funny outcome |
| **Fire Blast** | Coverage, so a Steel-type wall does not blank three quarters of the set |
| **Dragon Ascent** | Added when it Mega Evolves. Enormous, and it drops its own defences |

It runs the smart-trainer AI plus move prediction.

### Anti-cheese

- **Sleep, paralysis and freeze do not stop the fight.** Nothing Rayquaza does to the sky is a
  *move*. A sleeping Rayquaza still climbs, still builds Pressure, still vanishes and still lands
  Skyfall on your head — *"Rayquaza drifts on the wind… but the sky still turns."* Status buys you
  its **attack**; it buys you nothing against the **sky**.
- **Protect does not stop Skyfall.** It is not a move and cannot be blocked.
- **Stalling makes it worse.** Every turn you do not spend bringing it down is a turn the Pressure
  clock runs.
- **OHKO moves, Endeavor, Super Fang, Seismic Toss, Pain Split, Destiny Bond and Perish Song** are
  all shut out.
- **Ice will not carry the fight.** The type cap holds the 4x weakness at 2x, and Mega Rayquaza's
  strong winds take it to neutral outright.
- **Toxic does not ramp.** Its damage is flat for the whole encounter.
