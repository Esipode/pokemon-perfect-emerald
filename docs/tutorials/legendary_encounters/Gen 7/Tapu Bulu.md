## Tapu Bulu — The Land Spirit Pokémon

A guardian that grows stronger the longer the vegetation around it is left standing. Burn it down
and Tapu Bulu gets faster and hits harder - but loses the guard that growth built up.

### GROWTH — 0 to 6

Climbs on its own each turn while the fight continues. At its thresholds:

- **2** — Roots coil around your Pokémon's feet. Trapped, and Speed drops a stage.
- **3** — Overgrowth thickens. Tapu Bulu's guard hardens and it starts healing a little every turn.

### WRATH — 0 to 5

Only rises when you burn the Roots or the Overgrowth away with a Fire-type move. Doing so strips
whatever it hit, but:

- **3** — Wrath consumes it. Attack rises, and its guard drops for good.
- **5** — Forest Reclamation. Roots surge back even if you already burned them off once, and Speed
  drops a second stage.

### Phase 1 — Nature Guardian (100% → 50%)

Growth and Wrath both live. Leave the vegetation alone for a safer, tankier fight; burn it for a
faster, riskier one.

### Phase 2 — Ancient Guardian (≤50%)

Growth is forced to 4 or higher and the Roots snap back if they aren't already up.

### Phase 3 — The Forest Awakens (≤20%, the catch window)

Growth maxes out, guard peaks, and Tapu Bulu unleashes one last burst of damage. From there Growth
fades a step each turn - once it hits zero, the forest falls silent and it's safe to throw a Poké
Ball.

### What actually beats it

Decide early: leave the vegetation up for an easier, longer fight, or burn it down for a shorter,
harder one. Either way costs something - there's no free way to strip its guard.

### What does not work

Burning Roots or Overgrowth over and over without a plan - every use pushes Wrath toward Reckless
Guardian, and that trade isn't reversible once it flips.
