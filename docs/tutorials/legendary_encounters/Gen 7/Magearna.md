## Magearna — The Artificial Pokémon

A machine running four systems in sequence, never more than one (two, later) at a time. Watch
which one is live, then decide whether to break it or work around it.

### SYSTEMS — Defense / Offense / Repair / Energy

Only the announced system is doing anything. Landing a hit while Defense is up, or Magearna
attacking while Offense is up, strains that system; Repair and Energy strain themselves just by
running. Push a system hard enough and it **overloads** - shuts off until Magearna cycles back to
it. Defense overloading is your best window to swing hard; feeding Offense or Energy on purpose
usually isn't worth it.

### Phase 1 — Soul-Heart (100% → 60%)

One system online, rotating through Defense → Offense → Repair → Energy every few turns, always
announced a turn ahead. If Energy is about to overload, Magearna starts charging a **Heart
Pulse** - break Energy before it resolves and the charge fizzles.

### Phase 2 — Soul-Heart Awakening (60% → 40%)

A second system comes online opposite the first (Defense runs with Repair, Offense with Energy)
and both rotate together from here. Twice the systems to watch, twice the overloads to chase.

### Phase 3 — Emergency Protocol (40% → 10%)

Magearna Mega Evolves and starts giving systems up for good, one every few turns, in a fixed
order - each one buys it a one-time surge on the way out. Poké Balls are still blocked.

### Phase 4 — Soul-Heart Overdrive (≤10%, the catch window)

Its Mega form gives out - it's spent, not empowered. Now is the moment - Poké Balls work. Every
hit that lands collapses one more system; once all four are down, Fleur Cannon fires as its last
shot and the fight is yours to finish.

### What actually beats it

Track the announced system and press whichever one's overload helps you most. Break Energy before
a Heart Pulse resolves. In Overdrive, just keep landing hits - four is all it takes.

### What does not work

Ignoring the online callout - each system rewards a different response, and playing the same way
against all four leaves damage on the table. Trying to ball it before Overdrive.
