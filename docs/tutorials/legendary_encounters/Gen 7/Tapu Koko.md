## Tapu Koko — The Land Spirit Pokémon

A trickster that reads the pace of the fight and hits back exactly as hard as you push it. The more
it likes what it's seeing, the faster and harder it gets.

### VOLTAGE — 0 to 5

Its temper. Rises when it lands a hit, when Electric Terrain is up at the start of your turn, and
when you use an Electric move of your own. Falls when you outspeed it and when you outlast one of
its Challenges. The higher it climbs, the more dangerous everything below gets.

### Phase 1 — Lightning Guardian (100% → 50%)

Landing a hit on you while its temper is hot enough marks your active Pokémon with an **Electric
Charge** - a lightning strike is coming for it one turn later. A Ground-type move grounds the
charge out safely; riding it out costs a real hit.

Every so often it calls a **Speed Challenge**: it drags out whichever of your remaining Pokémon is
fastest. Knock that Pokémon out while the Challenge is open and its temper flares; keep it alive
until the Challenge passes and Koko backs off a little.

### Phase 2 — Guardian State (50% → 20%)

**Thunder Call** unlocks - a lightning strike telegraphed two turns ahead. There's no dodging in
place; the only way to duck it is to switch out before it lands.

Speed Challenges become **Guardian's Challenges** instead: Koko locks onto whoever's already on the
field. Using a status move while one is open gets punished - it costs a chunk of HP and stokes its
temper further. Outlasting it still calms Koko down.

### Phase 3 — Island Guardian (20% → 10%)

Its temper is maxed and staying there. Electric Terrain locks in for good, its Speed spikes hard,
and it keeps an Electric Charge running on you at all times - the instant one strikes, another
starts building.

### Phase 4 — Spent (≤10%, the catch window)

Koko gathers everything it has left, and a turn later its storm gives out entirely. Its guard drops
hard, every hazard it raised clears, and it's finally safe to throw a Poké Ball.

### What actually beats it

Keep a Ground-type answer ready for the Electric Charge. Don't feed it Electric moves of your own.
Switch out the instant Thunder Call is announced. Push through a Challenge rather than letting your
called-out Pokémon fall.

### What does not work

Stalling with status moves once a Guardian's Challenge is open - it just costs you HP. Ignoring
Electric Terrain and hoping its temper cools on its own; only outpacing it or outlasting a Challenge
brings it back down.
