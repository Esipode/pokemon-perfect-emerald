## Zeraora — The Thunderclap Pokémon

A speedster that feeds on tempo. Every hit it lands, every turn it wins, every fresh face it meets
hands it momentum - and once its momentum is high enough, it stops waiting its turn.

### MOMENTUM — 0 to 10

Its speed and aggression. Rises when it hits you, when your Pokémon faints, when you switch in,
and when Electric Terrain is up. Falls when you hit it while also having moved first, when its own
attack whiffs, and when it gets paralyzed. Landing Plasma Fists on you is worth even more to it.

At 6+ Momentum it may unleash **Thunderclap**: every move it has turns into a Quick Attack for one
turn, so whatever it picks goes first no matter what. Deny it Momentum and Thunderclap never comes
up.

Landing hit after hit builds a **Speed Chain** - three in a row and its crit chance spikes for as
long as the streak holds. Hit it back once and the chain breaks.

Its hits also fill a **Lightning Network** (0-5); your hits drain it. A full Network hands Zeraora
a burst of Momentum and resets. Keep landing hits to starve it.

### Phase 1 — Opening (100% → 71%)

The baseline loop above. Momentum only moves because of what happens in the fight - stalling
passively denies it nothing on its own.

### Phase 2 — Overdrive (≤70%)

Its Speed rises and Momentum starts trickling upward every turn on its own. The fight can no
longer be starved into stillness - press the attack instead of turtling.

### Phase 3 — Maximum Velocity (≤40%)

Zeraora Mega Evolves and Momentum maxes out. Whenever Momentum re-maxes from here on, it charges a
**Plasma Overload** - land a hit on it while charged and it discharges everything, a big mid-fight
swing. It isn't the catch window, though - it'll just charge again later if Momentum climbs back
to max.

### Phase 4 — Spent (≤10%, the catch window)

Its speed finally gives out. It reverts out of its Mega form and its guard drops hard - now's the
moment to throw a Poké Ball.

### What actually beats it

Outspeed it, hit it first, and keep hitting it. Every one of those denies Momentum. Break its
Speed Chain the moment it starts. Keep the pressure on all the way through Maximum Velocity so it
never gets the room to rebuild toward another Plasma Overload.

### What does not work

Switching around to stall it out - a fresh Pokémon walking in is free Momentum. Letting Electric
Terrain sit up. Ignoring a Speed Chain once it starts climbing.
