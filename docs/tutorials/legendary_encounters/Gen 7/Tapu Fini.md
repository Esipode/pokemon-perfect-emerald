## Tapu Fini — The Land Spirit Pokémon

A guardian that hides behind rising mist. The more of it there is, the harder Fini is to hit -
and the harder it hits you when it swings blind back.

### MIST — 0 to 5

Rises slowly on its own, and rises whenever a Water-type move lands on Fini. High Mist makes Fini
harder to hit and you easier to miss - a whiff at high Mist is just the fog swallowing the blow.

### Phase 1 — Water's Edge Guardian (100% → 50%)

Mist climbs quietly in the background. Every so often Fini calls on pure waters: it heals, cures
its own status, and washes away any hazards on its side - but the ritual costs it a point of Mist.

### Phase 2 — Guardian Mist (50% → 20%)

Fini slips deeper into the fog: its evasion rises and your accuracy drops, once, for good. From
here the mist periodically parts and offers a choice:

- **Step into it** — Fini's guard drops hard for one turn. A real window to hit it, at the cost of
  taking its move at full strength.
- **Hold your ground** — safer, but the mist only thickens.

### Phase 3 — Sacred Waters (≤20%, the catch window)

Mist maxes out and Fini slips fully into Sacred Mist, evasion climbing again while it heals every
other turn. That can't last - once it burns off, every stat change on the field (yours and Fini's)
is wiped clean and Guardian's Surge lands one huge final hit. After that, Fini stands exposed.

Separately, whenever Fini drops to 10% HP or below - in any phase - "Now is the moment!" opens the
catch window: Poké Balls start working and Fini's guard drops hard.

### What actually beats it

Hit it with something other than Water - Water moves feed the very mist that's making Fini hard to
hit. Push through Phase 1 fast, before the mist has time to compound, and take the Mist Trial's
risk when it's offered; it's the fight's cleanest damage window.

### What does not work

Leaning on Water-type attacks. Every hit that lands thickens the mist further, digging the hole
you're trying to hit out of.
