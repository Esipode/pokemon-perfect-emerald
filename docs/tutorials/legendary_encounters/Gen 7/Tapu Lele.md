## Tapu Lele — The Land Spirit Pokémon

A guardian that bends the battlefield around itself the longer the fight runs. The world gets
stranger in fixed, telegraphed steps - and every step it takes is permanent.

### DISTORTION — 0 to 5

Climbs when Tapu Lele uses a Psychic move, and just from turns passing (turns 5, 10, 15). Each
threshold locks in a new standing rule the instant it's crossed, and none of them go away on their
own:

- **1** — Psychic Terrain rises.
- **3** — You're trapped. No switching, no fleeing.
- **4** — Wonder Room: Defense and Special Defense swap roles.
- **5** — Trick Room: turn order reverses.

Every hit you land is also filed away as a resistance - the type that keeps working stops working
as well the next time you use it.

### Phase 1 — Psychic Guardian (100% → 50%)

Distortion climbs as above. Watch for a stat-change message that doesn't match what actually
happened once Distortion hits 2 - Tapu Lele is bluffing about which of its stats moved.

### Phase 2 — Psychic Reverie (≤50%)

Distortion locks at 5 - every standing rule above is live at once. Every three turns the world
lurches again: a sandstorm, a stat swing, or a tailwind favoring Tapu Lele. Watch for the recurring
line and adapt to whichever one shows up.

### Phase 3 — Guardian's Dream (≤10%, the catch window)

The world lurches every single turn for three turns straight. On the third, Tapu Lele delivers one
last hit and then its dream collapses completely - every rule clears, its guard drops, and it's
finally safe to throw a Poké Ball.

### What actually beats it

Vary your attack types so Mirrored Resonance can't get comfortable with any one of them. Get in
early, before Distortion 3 traps you in place. Once the world starts lurching, read the recurring
line and play around whatever it just changed rather than fighting the old rules.

### What does not work

Leaning on one attack type once it's been read once. Trying to switch out at Distortion 3 or higher.
Trusting a stat-change message at face value once the mirages start.
