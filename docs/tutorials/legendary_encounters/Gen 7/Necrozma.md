## Necrozma — The Prism Pokémon

A starving light-eater. It grows stronger the more you feed it, and weaker the more you starve it.
Two mid-fight transformations wait at 50% and 20% HP, and neither is under your control.

### LIGHT — 0 to 100, only ever rises

Necrozma's hunger. It climbs when you heal, when you land a big hit, or when you ignore a Light
Fragment. At 70+ it crits more. At 90+ it gets a one-time power boost. Feed it less: keep hits
modest, don't over-heal, and don't let Fragments go to waste.

### Phase 0 — Starved (100% → 71%)

#### Light Fragments
A shard of light appears every so often and lingers for two turns. Land any hit on Necrozma before
it fades and you claim it, chipping a little more HP off. Ignore it and Necrozma eats it, growing
hungrier.

### Phase 1 — The Fusion (≤70%)

A coin flip decides what Necrozma becomes. No way to influence which.

#### Dusk Mane
Wears three layers of Solar Armor. Each layer shatters as its HP crosses 40%, 30%, and 20% — every
break weakens its guard and strengthens Sunsteel Strike. Keep the pressure on to strip it faster.

#### Dawn Wings
An eclipse deepens on its own clock, growing its special attack in stages. It never gets easier on
its own — the eclipse doesn't reverse, but it also can't overwhelm you outright.

### Phase 2 — Ultra Necrozma (≤40%, the catch window at ≤10%)

Light resets and starts draining every turn instead of rising. Photon Geyser is telegraphed one
turn ahead when the warning fires — the category is certain, the exact turn of impact only an
approximation. At 10% HP its light gives out and the Poké Ball works.

### What actually beats it

Steady, moderate damage. Save big heals for when you can afford the Light spike. Always contest a
Light Fragment — claiming is strictly better than letting it feed the boss.

### What does not work

Stalling it out with status alone — Toxic ticks flat here, it never ramps to something lethal.
Trying to outlast the eclipse by doing nothing — it only ever climbs.
