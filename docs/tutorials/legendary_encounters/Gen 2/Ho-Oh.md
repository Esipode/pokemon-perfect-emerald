## Ho-Oh — The Rainbow Above the Ashes

Ho-Oh fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage.

None of that is the fight. The fight is that **everything Ho-Oh does for you is paid for out of its
own resurrection.**

### Sacred Flame — the number the whole fight runs on

Ho-Oh carries a hidden resource, 0 to 8, and it starts at **3**. It is three things at once:

- its **damage reduction**,
- its entire **heal budget**,
- and its **second life**.

You never see the number. You see how its fire is described. Ho-Oh reckons its fire up twice a
turn - at the top of the turn and at the end of it - and tells you whenever the reading has changed
since the last one:

| Line | Flame |
| --- | --- |
| *Ho-Oh's fire has burned down to nothing.* | 0 |
| *Ho-Oh's fire is running thin.* | 1 |
| *Ho-Oh's fire burns steady again.* | 2–4 |
| *Ho-Oh's fire climbs higher than before!* | 5–8 |

At the extremes a standing reminder runs every turn as well — *Only embers are left in Ho-Oh's
plumage.* / *Ho-Oh is carrying more fire than it arrived with.*

### The inversion — every kindness costs it

**Ho-Oh is not hostile in the way the other legendaries are.** It mends, purifies, blesses, revives
and rekindles — and each of those is flame off its own rebirth.

| It spends flame when… | Cost | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| it lost **6% or more of its HP** this turn (needs 2 flame) | −2 | *Ho-Oh bathes itself in sacred fire!* — heals 4% |
| it is **statused** at the end of a turn (needs 1) | −1 | *The sacred flame burns the sickness away!* |
| you **overwrote the sun** and it takes it back | −1 | *Ho-Oh spreads its wings and calls the sunlight back!* |
| it **blesses** one of your Pokémon | −2 | a named blessing banner |
| it **revives** one of your fallen | −3 | *Sacred fire pours into one of your fallen Pokémon.* |

| It gains flame when… | Gain |
| --- | --- |
| it uses **Sacred Fire** (once per turn) | +1 |
| one of **your Pokémon faints** (once per turn) | +2 |
| the **sun is up** at the end of a turn, once the rainbow is out | +1 |
| the **rainbow arrives** | +2 |
| it judges you **Impure** or **Defiant** | +1 |

So the fight has two viable lines and they lead to visibly different battles:

- **Hit hard.** Ho-Oh mends constantly, the fight runs long, and the flame drains.
- **Chip gently.** The fight is short — and Ho-Oh reaches the end with a rebirth still in the tank.

### The guard ladder

The damage reduction moves with the flame, so you can read the resource straight off the damage
numbers. It is fixed for the whole of a turn - the guard you were told about at the top of the turn
is the guard you face until the turn ends:

| | Flame 0–1 | Flame 2–4 | Flame 5–8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Rainbow Pokémon** (100–50%) | 82% | 84% | 88% |
| **The Rainbow** (50% → the floor) | 84% | 88% | 90% |
| **Divine Rebirth** (55–10%) | 80% | flame is gone | |
| **Weakened** (10% and below) | 74% | | |

It is recomputed at the same two points, and announced whenever it has moved:

> *The sacred flames close around Ho-Oh — it is harder to reach!*
> *Ho-Oh's light dims, and its guard with it.*

### The Rainbow (at 50%)

A rainbow opens and **permanent sun** comes up. Two separate things, and the difference matters.

- **The sun is ordinary weather.** Any weather move of yours overwrites it, which cuts the +1/turn
  flame income. Ho-Oh buys it back at the top of the next turn for one flame — so blowing the sun
  out costs you a turn and costs Ho-Oh a piece of its resurrection. It also swings **Weather Ball**
  between 50 BP Normal and 100 BP Fire.
- **The rainbow itself is not weather.** Nothing removes it. It **doubles Ho-Oh's secondary-effect
  chances** — Sacred Fire's 50% burn becomes near certain, and Extrasensory's flinch doubles.

You can dim the rainbow's *income*. You cannot dim its *danger*.

### Sacred Judgment — every four turns

You get a turn's warning: *Ho-Oh's gaze settles on you...*

Then **HO-OH JUDGES YOUR ACTIONS.** The verdict is decided in this order — the first one that
matches wins, so a player who is both impure and down a Pokémon gets no mercy:

| Verdict | You get it when | Ho-Oh does | Flame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **IMPURE** | Ho-Oh is **statused** when the judgment lands | Hazes both sides, cures itself for free | **+1** |
| **DEFIANT** | any of your stats at **+2 or more**, evasion at **+1**, or **2+ switches** since the last judgment | +1 Atk and +1 Sp. Atk to itself | **+1** |
| **MERCIFUL** | you have **lost a Pokémon** since the last judgment | **revives** a fallen bench Pokémon at 50% HP | **−3** |
| **WORTHY** | none of the above | grants you a **blessing** | **−2** |

The two verdicts that punish you are the two that *feed* it. Playing badly is not just punished
locally — it actively funds the rebirth.

**Statusing Ho-Oh is worth doing, and it has a rhythm.** Poison it right after a judgment and you
get three free purifications out of its flame. Poison it right *before* one and you hand it a flame
and eat a Haze.

### Blessings — and their price

A Worthy verdict picks one of four at random and lays it on your active Pokémon for **three turns**:

| Blessing | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Swiftness** | +2 Speed |
| **Valor** | +1 Attack, +1 Sp. Attack |
| **Aegis** | +1 Defense, +1 Sp. Defense |
| **Purity** | a standing **Safeguard**, and your status is cured |

Purity is not filler: with the rainbow up, Sacred Fire burns on nearly every connect, so a standing
Safeguard is the most valuable thing Ho-Oh can hand you.

**The price.** Every turn the blessing is active, the blessed Pokémon loses **7% of its max HP**:

> *The flame that blesses is also consuming!*

**Refusing it.** Switching out sheds the blessing on the spot — *The sacred flame gutters as your
Pokémon leaves the field.* You lose the stat stages anyway when you switch, so the trade is honest:
give up the boon to stop the burn, at the cost of a turn. But switches are also what the **Defiant**
verdict counts, so you cannot do it forever.

### The floor — Rebirth or Ashes

Ho-Oh cannot be knocked out before the rainbow phase ends. The blow that would do it takes it to 1 HP instead, its
flames go out, and the field goes silent. Then the flame decides the fight:

| Flame left | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **4 or more** | **DIVINE REBIRTH.** A rainbow opens, Ho-Oh returns at **30% HP**, and you fight a whole extra phase |
| **3 or less** | **ASHES.** It reaches for a fire that isn't there. Straight to **8% HP** and the catch window |

Draining the flame is rewarded with a **shorter fight and an immediate catch window**. The optimal
line is also the fast line — and you are never locked out of catching it either way.

### Divine Rebirth — the Rite

The flame is gone for good. No more mending, no more purification, no more judgments, no more
blessings — Ho-Oh stops reacting to you at all and starts performing a liturgy on a four-turn loop:

| Beat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **SACRED FLAME** | +10% to its **raw** Attack and Sp. Attack — this compounds every cycle |
| **PURIFICATION** | Hazes **both sides**, strips every screen, cures itself |
| **JUDGMENT** | 12% of your Pokémon's max HP, straight through Protect and screens |
| **PHOENIX** | 12%, likewise |

The asymmetry is the phase's teeth: Ho-Oh's gains are raw stat values, so its own Purification does
not undo them, while **yours are wiped every fourth turn.** You have to close this out, not stall it.

### Catching it

Poké Balls are blocked until Ho-Oh is **weakened** — reached either from Ashes immediately, or from
Divine Rebirth at 10% HP:

> *The last of the sacred flame goes out, and Ho-Oh settles to the ground.*
> *It's spent — now is the moment to catch it!*

At that point the guard drops to 76%, the move immunities come off, and the catch rate is set.

### What it fights with

| Move | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Sacred Fire** | its signature, and the escalation cue — under the rainbow the burn is near guaranteed |
| **Weather Ball** | 50 BP Normal with no weather, 100 BP Fire under the sun. The reason to care about the sky |
| **Earthquake** | coverage into the Rock and Electric answers you will bring for a Fire/Flying boss |
| **Extrasensory** | Psychic coverage into Fighting, a flinch chance the rainbow doubles — and the one move that does **not** feed the flame |

### Anti-cheese

Ho-Oh has **no blanket status immunity at all** — unusually for a legendary. Status lands and
matters; it is folded into the resource economy rather than walled off. Toxic is bounded by flat
damage rather than immunity, setup and evasion are answered by the Defiant verdict and then by the
Rite's Purification, pivot-stalling is answered by Defiant's switch count, and Protect and screens
stop none of the scripted chip.
