## Lugia — The Storm Beneath the Sea

### Location: Navel Rock

Lugia fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage.

None of that is the fight. The fight is that **you have to make this battle worse on purpose.**

### The Storm — the number the whole fight runs on

Lugia keeps one number, and it is not hidden behind a script: it is the **weather**. Lugia raises it
itself, roughly one rung a turn, by attacking.

| Rung | What you see | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | *The sky goes quiet again.* | nothing |
| 1 | *A cold wind starts to pick up.* | nothing |
| 2 | **Rain.** *The clouds break, and the rain comes down hard!* | Lugia's Thunder can no longer miss; its Hydro Pump hits 1.5x |
| 3 | **Storm.** *The rain turns to a howling storm!* | the field starts chipping you every turn |
| 4 | **Tempest.** *A TEMPEST tears across the field!* | **Electric, Ice and Rock stop being super effective against Lugia.** Heavier chip |
| 5 | **Maelstrom.** *The sea itself climbs into the sky!* | heaviest chip, −1 accuracy every turn, and switching in costs 10% |

Rungs 4 and 5 are primal-class weather. **Nothing you have can overwrite them** — the door to the
weather closes as the ladder climbs.

How it moves:

| Direction | Cause | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up | Lugia used **Aeroblast** | +2 |
| Up | Lugia used **Hydro Pump** or **Thunder** | +1 |
| Up | a phase transition | +1 |
| Up | the Maelstrom's dive strike resolves | +2 |
| Down | **you overwrite the weather while the storm is at rung 2–3** | −2 |
| Down | the **Eye** opens at the end of a Maelstrom cycle | to 0 |
| Down | Lugia **loses control** | to 1 |

Only one rise lands per turn, so a Psychic turn is a free turn.

### The inversion — calm Lugia is *harder*

Read the guard table before you decide to play it safe:

| | Storm 0–1 | Storm 2–3 | Storm 4 | Storm 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guardian of the Sea** (100–50%) | 86% | 89% | — | — |
| **The Sea Erupts** (50–25%) | 84% | 88% | 92% | 95% |
| **Ocean's Wrath** (25–10%) | 82% | 87% | 91% | 94% |
| **The Eye is open** | 94% | | | |
| **Lugia has lost control** | **70%** | | | |
| **Weakened** (10% and below) | 76% | | | |

The guard climbs with the storm, so the obvious plan is to keep venting it. That plan works, and it
loses. **A calm Lugia never tires.** You will be at 84–86% reduction for the entire fight and
nothing else will ever happen.

Every time the guard actually moves you are told:

> *The sea closes around Lugia — it is harder to reach!*
> *Lugia's guard slips.*

Those lines re-fire on every real change, so you can always read where you stand.

### The Strain — the race you are actually running

While the storm is at rung 4 or 5, **Lugia is straining against the sea it raised**, and it says so:

> *Lugia's wings falter against the sea it raised.*
> *Lugia is fighting the storm as hard as it is fighting you!*

Strain builds from these, and only these:

| Source | Strain |
| --- | --- |
| Ending a turn with the storm at rung 4 or 5 | +1 (+2 in Ocean's Wrath) |
| The Maelstrom's dive strike resolving | +1 |
| **You took ≥ 6% of Lugia's health off in one turn**, while the storm is at 4+ | +2 (≥ 8% → +3 in Ocean's Wrath) |
| Every turn the **Eye** is open | −2 |

At **6**, Lugia loses hold of it:

> *Lugia struggles against the raging sea it summoned!*

The storm collapses to rung 1, Lugia takes 7% self-damage, and its guard falls to **70% for two full
turns**. It is the only place in this fight where big damage happens.

**This is why turtling loses.** The cycle sheds more Strain in its Eye than it builds on its own —
the heavy-damage bonus is the difference. If you spend the Maelstrom hiding behind Protect and
healing, Strain never caps and you have achieved nothing except surviving.

While the window is open you are reminded every turn:

> *Lugia still hasn't recovered its rhythm.*

### The Maelstrom cycle

The moment the storm reaches rung 5 (from The Sea Erupts onward), Lugia arms a five-turn cycle:

> *LUGIA SUMMONED A MAELSTROM!*

| Turn | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Surface** | full Maelstrom chip, accuracy erosion |
| **Deep Sea** | *Lugia vanished beneath the waves!* — it is **untargetable this turn** and still attacks. *The ocean begins to churn violently!* |
| **The Strike** | the sea erupts under your Pokémon for **22% of its max HP**. Not a move — Protect and screens do not stop it |
| **The Eye opens** | *The storm goes eerily calm...* — the storm drops to 0 |
| **Eye (2 turns)** | no chip, Lugia mends 4% a turn, guard hardens to 94%, **Strain drains −2 a turn** |
| **The eye closes** | *The eye begins to close!* — the storm comes back at rung 3 (rung 4 in Ocean's Wrath) |

The Deep Sea turn is pure pressure: you cannot hurt Lugia, it can still hurt you. Heal, switch or
set up — the strike lands next turn.

**The Eye is not a damage window.** At 94% it is the hardest Lugia ever is. It is a
heal-and-reposition window, and it is where Strain runs backwards, so every Eye turn undoes work.

**The one thing worth doing in the Eye:** set your own weather. The storm cannot stop you while it is
at 0, and if the field still has weather on it when the eye closes, the storm comes back at **rung 1
instead of rung 3 or 4**. It is the only discount in the fight.

### The vent — a panic button, not a strategy

While the storm is at rung 2–3, overwriting the weather knocks two rungs off it:

> *The sky tears open, and the sea loses its rhythm!*
> *Everything the weather was doing is gone — yours as well.*

Read that second line carefully. Venting from 2–3 always lands at 0–1, which is **no weather at
all** — your Sunny Day goes with Lugia's rain. It buys you safety from the chip and it costs every
point of progress toward a collapse.

Use it when a Pokémon has to survive one more turn. Do not use it as a plan.

### Tempest — your coverage is rearranged, not walled

Lugia is Psychic/Flying, so the types you would naturally bring are Electric, Ice, Rock, Ghost and
Dark. Strong Winds at rung 4 clamps super-effective hits on a Flying-type back to neutral, which
cancels **exactly** its Flying weaknesses:

- **Electric, Ice, Rock** — neutral from rung 4 up.
- **Ghost, Dark** — still 2x. They hit the Psychic half, which Strong Winds does not protect.

So bring the other half of your coverage. A Dark or Ghost attacker keeps working through the entire
storm, which is precisely the window where damage matters.

### What does and doesn't stick

- **Sleep and Freeze only stick while the storm is below rung 2.** From rung 2 up Lugia sheds them
  at the top of every turn (*The raging sea will not let Lugia rest!*). You can sleep-lock it — after
  venting the storm to nothing, which is exactly the trade the fight is about. Note that the shed
  cures **everything**, so putting a storming Lugia to sleep also throws away your Toxic.
- **Burn, poison and paralysis stick at every rung.** Toxic is bounded (flat damage, no ramp).
- **Protect and screens do not stop the Maelstrom chip or the dive strike.** Both are scripted, not
  moves.
- **Pivot stalling is taxed.** At rung 5, every Pokémon you send in loses 10% on entry (*The undertow
  drags at your Pokémon!*).
- **Water-types ignore the chip entirely** (*The sea does not touch its own.*) — but Lugia carries
  Thunder for exactly that reason.
- **Flying-types take roughly 1.5x the chip** — the wind tears them out of the air.
- **Accuracy erosion at rung 5** is answerable with Haze or Mist, or by riding the cycle out: the Eye
  is a free switch, and a switch resets stages.
- **OHKO, fixed-damage, Pain Split and Perish Song** are shut out entirely.

### Lugia's moveset

- **Aeroblast** — its signature, high crit rate, and the ladder's biggest single push at +2.
- **Hydro Pump** — rain-boosted from rung 2 up, so the ladder makes it visibly hit harder. Answers
  the Rock-types you bring for the Flying half.
- **Thunder** — **cannot miss from rung 2 up**, and it is what punishes the Water-types who otherwise
  walk through the Maelstrom untouched.
- **Psychic** — the only move that does *not* raise the storm.

### Phases and catching it

1. **Guardian of the Sea** (above 50% HP) — guard **86–89%**. The storm is capped at rung 3, so no
   Tempest and no Maelstrom yet. This is where you learn the ladder.
2. **The Sea Erupts** (50% HP or below) — guard **82–90%**. The cap comes off: Tempest, Maelstrom and
   Strain all go live, with half the fight still left to learn them in.
3. **Ocean's Wrath** (25% HP or below) — guard **80–89%**. The storm is floored at rung 4, Strain
   builds twice as fast, and the heavy-damage bonus triggers at 4% instead of 6%. Lugia cannot be
   knocked out here.
4. **Weakened** (10% HP or below) — the storm falls out of the sky. Every mechanic switches off, the
   guard drops to 70%, and **Poké Balls work**. The only catch window. Its catch rate is generous
   here, and the catch-phase safety net (see
   [Common to every legendary](#common-to-every-legendary)) keeps Lugia alive through your hits.
