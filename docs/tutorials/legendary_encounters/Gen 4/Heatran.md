## Heatran — The Lava Dome

Heatran fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage. It also **cannot be taken below 1 HP** until the very end, so the catch window is guaranteed
no matter how the damage race goes.

The 2x cap earns its keep here more than anywhere else in the set: Heatran is **Fire / Steel**, which
takes **4x from Ground** on paper. One Earthquake would otherwise cut straight through the guard.
That shortcut is closed.

It keeps its own ability, **Flash Fire** — so fighting fire with fire is off the table from turn one.

Every other legendary asks how hard you can hit. Heatran does not really fight you at all.
**The mountain does.**

> **The only turns in this battle where your damage means anything are the turns right after the
> volcano goes off. You have to make that happen — and it costs you about a fifth of your team every
> time.**

### VOLCANIC PRESSURE

One hidden number runs the whole fight, and **both ends of it hurt you.**

```
   0          1          2          3          4          5
 dormant     warm      heated     MOLTEN    erupting     VENT
   |          |          |          |          |          |
   -      Heatran     the floor   LAVA FLOW  a Magma    the mountain
        gets meaner    burns      + sunlight  Pocket     lets go
                       you        + the field  every
                                  scoured      turn

 guard 88 ------------------------- 92 -------------------> 76 for three turns
```

Let it climb and the ground starts killing you, pockets open under your feet, and the sun comes up —
which halves your Water moves, the coolant *and* your best damage type. Hold it at 0 and nothing
happens to you at all, but you just spent every turn cooling instead of attacking, and the vent you
refused to let happen was the only real damage window in the fight.

Nothing on screen ever shows you this number. What it shows you is **the dome**, and the dome
describes the number honestly, every step of the way:

| | The mountain says |
| --- | --- |
| **5 — vent** | *"EVERYTHING GOES STILL. The mountain draws breath."* |
| **4 — erupting** | *"The mountain is boiling, and Heatran has stopped getting out of its way."* |
| **3 — Molten** | *"THE BASIN IS RUNNING OVER."* |
| **2 — heated** | *"The floor of the dome is glowing through its cracks."* |
| **1 — warm** | *"Something turns over far below, and the air over the dome goes dry."* |
| **0 — dormant** | *"The mountain settles, and the stone underfoot stops humming."* |

Every crossing is announced, in both directions. Underneath them, once the lava is up, a short line
keeps running every other turn — *"The lava is still moving across your side of the field."*

### What moves it

| Raises it (+) | Lowers it (−) |
| --- | --- |
| **Heatran uses a Fire move** — +1, once a turn | Your **Ice-type move** — **−1** |
| **Turns passing** — +1 every 2 turns, then +1/turn, then +2/turn | Your **Water-type move** — **−2** |
| **A hit worth 15%+ of its max HP** in one turn — +1 | **Rain, hail or snow standing** — −1 a turn |
| **A status standing on Heatran** — +1, *every turn it lasts* | |

Type is read off the **move's printed type**, not what it hit as — a Normalize'd, Electrified or
Tera'd attack still counts as whatever the move data says it is.

### THERMAL SHOCK — why Water is a trap

Water is 2x on Fire/Steel. It is your best damage type and your fastest coolant, at two stages a hit.
And taking two stages off the dial in one action is exactly what the rock punishes:

> *"The rock around Heatran contracts with a crack, and settles harder than it was."*
>
> **Heatran's guard goes to 96% for three turns.** Anything you throw at it in that window is worth
> roughly a third of what it would have been.

It only fires when the drop actually removed two stages — so a Water move at Pressure 0 or 1 is safe.
**Ice never triggers it, ever.** You always have a safe way to cool the mountain; it is just half as
fast. At Molten the sun is up and halving your Water anyway, which is the turn Ice quietly becomes
the correct answer.

While the shock is up, a line keeps running: *"Heatran's shell is still drawn tight."*

### MAGMA POCKETS

Once the floor is glowing (Pressure 2+), the ground under your active Pokémon lights up on a timer.

```
  "The ground beneath <MON> begins to glow!"
        |
        +--  a turn passes   "The glow under <MON> is getting brighter!"
        +--  a turn passes   "…getting brighter!"
        +--  it opens        16% of your Pokémon's max HP
```

They come every third turn while Heatran is dormant, **every second turn** in the Magma Core, and
**every turn** once the basin is boiling over.

**Three ways out, and you can always take one of them:**

| Out | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Switch out** | *"The magma comes up through ground nobody is standing on."* |
| **Protect** (or anything Protect-like) | *"The burst breaks against <MON>'s guard!"* |
| **Cool the dial below Molten** before it opens | *"The glow under <MON> dims, and goes out."* |

None of that is free, because **Magma Storm is in its moveset.** It traps. While it holds, the switch
is gone and you are standing on a lit pocket with only Protect or the dial left.

### The field itself

| Pressure | What the ground does to you each turn |
| --- | --- |
| **2** | ~6% — *"The heat coming up through the floor scalds your side!"* |
| **3+** | The **Sea of Fire** burns your whole side, plus another 5% if your Pokémon cannot get off the ground |

That last one reads **Flying-types and Levitate** and nothing else — Air Balloon and Magnet Rise will
not save you from it.

Molten also **scours every entry hazard off both sides.** Anything you set is gone; so is the Stealth
Rock Heatran laid.

### THE VENT — the loop the whole fight turns on

At Pressure 5, in the first two phases, the mountain goes off on its own:

```
  "THE DOME VENTS."

    -> the blast takes 8% off your side (12% in the Magma Core)
    -> Pressure resets to 0; the lava and the sun come down
    -> Heatran's guard: 88 -> 84, its stats drop, and it LOSES ITS NEXT ACTION
                            ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
                    THREE TURNS. This is your damage window.
```

Your damage per turn swings roughly **fivefold** between waiting and this. That is what you are
buying, and the price is about a fifth of your team, every time. Racing the dial *up* is a real
decision, not a free button — and rain can genuinely stop a vent on the turn it would have happened,
which is the strongest thing weather control buys you here.

### Status: no immunity, but a price

Sleep, paralysis, Toxic — all of it lands on Heatran and all of it works completely. Sleep still
costs it turns; paralysis still costs it speed.

> *"Heatran has gone still — and the heat has nowhere to go."*
> **Every turn a status stands on Heatran, the Pressure gains an extra +1.**

A vent you did not choose to open still costs you 8–12% and still puts the lava up on the way there.
Toxic is the sharpest version of this and is deliberately left playable: bounded to a flat 1/16, it
becomes a permanent +1 a turn for the rest of the fight. That is a legitimate strategy — *live
dangerously, win faster* — not an exploit. It accelerates everything aimed at you exactly as fast as
it accelerates your windows.

Fire/Steel closes the rest by itself: Heatran cannot be burned, and Fire-types cannot be frozen.

### The three beats

**50% — MEGA EVOLUTION.** *"Heatran has taken the mountain into itself."* Heatran **Mega Evolves**,
**Overheat replaces Lava Plume**, the Pressure jumps two stages and now climbs **+1 every turn** on
its own. Pockets come every second turn. Vents hit for **12%** and drop its guard to 78.

Its raw Sp. Atk is pulled straight back down after the transformation — the phase's threat is meant
to come from the volcano, not from a stat line. What the Mega does keep is the defensive half:
**Sp. Def 106 → 141**, which is what the 91/94 guard is sitting on top of. Special attackers get
noticeably worse here.

And it brings one thing of its own — the reason this phase plays differently rather than just hitting
harder:

> **While the dial is at Molten or above, Mega Heatran heals 3% every turn.**
> *"Heatran is drinking from the flow."*

**This inverts everything the first half of the fight taught you.** Until now a hot dial was purely
your friend — it was how you bought a vent window. From here a dial left parked hot **pays Heatran
rent every single turn it sits there.** You have to time the climb now instead of just letting it
run.

The answer is the loop you already know: **venting resets the dial to 0**, so it closes the tap *and*
opens your window in the same beat. Sit at Molten waiting and you are healing it; drive it to 5 and
cash it in and you are not.

It stops at Eruption. Once the mountain has taken over from Heatran, nothing is drinking anything.

**16% — ERUPTION.** *"THE ENTIRE MOUNTAIN BEGINS TO SHAKE!"* The dial is slammed to Molten and climbs
**+2 every single turn.** Now you have to drive it **down**.

| You get it to | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **0** | *"The pressure under the dome lets go, and nothing comes up after it."* |
| **5** | **SUPERNOVA — about half your side's max HP**, and the field burns permanently |

Ice is −1, Water is −2 but shocks, rain is another −1 — so holding it flat needs two of the three,
and driving it down needs all of them. **Both endings leave Heatran spent** at guard 45, so failing
here is enormous but never locks you out of the catch. It is also the only place in the fight
something other than a move can knock your Pokémon out, so do not walk in on fumes.

**10% — WEAKENED.** Balls unlock. Heatran returns to its normal form, its guard drops, its status
clears, and the mountain is finished. Catch rate **40** — it is not a gift, but it is a window, and
it is the same window on every path.

### Its moves

`Magma Storm` · `Lava Plume` (`Overheat` from 50%) · `Earth Power` · `Stealth Rock`

All legal Heatran moves, played by the smart trainer AI. Two of the four are Fire — which is how the
dial climbs off its own attacks — and **Flash Fire** means your own Fire moves do nothing but feed it.

### If you take nothing else away

- The dial is the fight. Heatran's HP bar barely moves outside a vent window.
- **Water is your best damage type and your worst habit.** Learn where the 96% guard comes from.
- **Ice is always safe.** Slower, and correct more often than you would think — especially in the sun.
- Protect, and a spare switch-in, are worth a moveslot here.
- Letting the mountain go off is the plan. Doing it while your team can still take 20% is the skill.
- **After the Mega, a hot dial is no longer free.** Climb it to cash it, not to park it.
