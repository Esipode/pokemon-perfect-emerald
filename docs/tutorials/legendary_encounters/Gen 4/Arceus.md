## Arceus — The Original One

Arceus fights behind the usual legendary package: a heavy damage reduction, immunity to OHKO,
fixed-damage, HP-swap and shared-KO moves, incoming type effectiveness capped at 2x, and flat Toxic
damage.

Every other legendary has one idea. Regirock is a wall you pick a tool for; Registeel learns what
you keep hitting it with; Regice eats your turns. **Arceus is a rulebook that keeps rewriting
itself**, and every phase asks you for a different skill.

```
PHASE 0  The Creator             DECREES      adapt to a rule you did not agree to
PHASE 1  The Plates              PLATE LAWS   type knowledge, on a rotation
PHASE 2  The Creator's Judgment  + THE MARK   target management under a clock
PHASE 3  Creation                RESET        rebuild after everything is taken away
PHASE 4  The Original One        RAW POWER    general battle mastery
         + JUDGMENT: CREATION                 everything, at once, for five turns
PHASE 5  The Final Moment                     just Arceus.  Beat it yourself.
```

Underneath all of it is one number you never see printed — but which the fight narrates constantly.

---

### The spine: AUTHORITY

Arceus does not act for free. **Everything it does costs Authority**, it regains a little every
turn, and it can never act on credit.

```
            0 ----------------------------------------- 12
            ^ where you want it        where it wants it ^

Arceus REGAINS  +1 every turn.
Arceus SPENDS it to act:
    invoke a Plate .......... 1
    declare a Decree ........ 2
    begin a Judgment ........ 3

YOU push it down:
    break the active Plate .......... -2
    break Judgment's concentration .. -2
    disrupt an active Decree ........ -1
    shatter Judgment: Creation ...... -5
```

At 0 it can only attack — and when it reaches for something it cannot pay for, it says so:

> *Arceus reaches for something, and finds nothing there. Its authority wavers.*

**That line is the scoreboard.** So is the tier line, which fires every time the meter really moves:

| The line | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| *Arceus's presence thins. The air is only air.* | **0–3** |
| *Arceus's will settles over the field.* | 4–7 |
| *The world leans toward Arceus.* | 8–11 |
| *ARCEUS'S AUTHORITY IS ABSOLUTE.* | **12** |

Getting the meter to the bottom tier is not optional flavour. **It is the only thing that earns you
the CHALLENGE option in the finale**, and the check happens once, at one moment, on the whole
fight's worth of work.

Putting Arceus to sleep or freezing it does not help. It throws the status off — and the attempt
*feeds* the meter by 2.

---

### Phase 0 — The Creator (100% – 78%)

No Plates. Just **Decrees**: a field-wide rule Arceus declares for three turns at a time, for 2
Authority. It always announces them, and reminds you every turn one is up.

| Decree | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Strength** | +2 Attack to **everyone**, Arceus included. Undone when it expires |
| **Mind** | The same, for Sp. Atk |
| **Binding** | Nobody may switch out or flee |
| **Balance** | Every stat stage on the field is wiped, then Mist protects both sides for the duration |
| **Toll** | Every battler loses **6% of max HP every turn** — Arceus pays it too |

**You can break a Decree early.** Take **4% of Arceus's max HP off in a single turn** and it
collapses:

> *The Decree cracks under the pressure!*

That is worth 1 Authority, and it is the earliest lever you have on the meter.

Meanwhile — and this is never announced — Arceus is **watching what you attack it with**. Every
damaging move that lands is quietly filed as physical or special. Nothing tells you this is
happening. It matters at 34%.

---

### Phase 1 — The Plates (≤ 78%)

> *Arceus's ring begins to glow. The Plates surrounding its body resonate.*
> **THE PLATES AWAKEN.**

Arceus invokes a Plate. **This is a real form change** — its typing, its stats, its damage
calculation and the effectiveness message you see all change with it. Each Plate also rewrites its
fourth move with an attack of that type, and imposes a **law** on the field.

| Plate | It becomes | The law | Broken by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flame** | Fire | Harsh sunlight, and a **Sea of Fire** burning your side every turn | **Water** |
| **Splash** | Water | Rain, and a **Swamp** that quarters your Speed | **Electric** |
| **Zap** | Electric | **Tailwind** for Arceus, and your Speed drops a stage | **Ground** |
| **Meadow** | Grass | The field grows over you — your side loses **5% of max HP every turn** | **Fire** |
| **Icicle** | Ice | Snow, and **Aurora Veil** halving what you deal | **Fighting** |
| **Fist** | Fighting | **Mist** refuses your stat drops, and its Attack **climbs +1 every turn** | **Psychic** |

The rule you have to learn is the rule you already know: **hit it with what beats what it has
become.**

**Breaking a Plate is damage-independent.** A 20-power Water Gun tears down the Flame Plate exactly
as thoroughly as a Hydro Pump. The Plate is the lock, the type is the key, and how hard you turn it
does not matter — so an under-levelled party is never locked out of the answer.

A break costs Arceus 2 Authority, strips the law, drops it back to plain Normal-type Arceus with a
weak status move in the fourth slot, and **costs it a turn of tempo** on the rotation.

**The rotation is telegraphed.** When Arceus's ring turns, it tells you which Plate is coming *that
same turn*, before you choose your move — and the Plate lands at the end of it. The Plate on its way
out cannot be broken.

Cadence: a new Plate every **third** turn here.

---

### Phase 2 — The Creator's Judgment (≤ 55%)

> *Arceus raises its head. The Plates begin to orbit faster.*
> **ARCEUS HAS DECIDED TO JUDGE YOU.**

Plates now rotate every **second** turn, and a second clock starts running.

**The Divine Mark.** For 3 Authority, Arceus marks whichever Pokémon is on your field, and the mark
escalates one stage a turn:

```
1  MARKED     "Arceus's gaze has not left your POKéMON."
2  CONDEMNED  "Arceus's attacks begin to converge.  JUDGMENT NEARS."
              (+1 Attack and +1 Sp. Atk to Arceus for that turn)
3  JUDGMENT falls at the end of this turn - 35% of your max HP
```

**Three ways out, and they cost different things:**

| Out | How | What it costs you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Transfer** | Switch out | The mark rides whoever is on the field, so it drops back to stage 1. You bought two turns and paid a Pokémon's worth of tempo |
| **Disrupt** | Take **12% of Arceus's max HP** off in the turn Judgment would land | Nothing. And it costs Arceus **2 Authority** |
| **Break the Plate** | Any Plate break, at any stage | Nothing. **Breaking a Plate is always the right answer to something** |

Every Judgment that actually lands is followed by an **echo** of one of the other creation
legendaries, chosen by whichever Plate is up:

| Echo | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| *Time folds around Arceus.* | Tailwind for Arceus, four turns |
| *Space closes. There is nowhere to go.* | You are trapped — no switching, no fleeing |
| *The world turns inside out.* | The weather is wiped, and so is everything you had up on your side |

---

### Phase 3 — Creation (≤ 34%)

> *Arceus looks toward the heavens.*
> *For a moment, there is nothing.*
> *A spark. Then light.*
> **Arceus has recreated the world.**

This is a genuine hard reset. **Weather, screens, Sea of Fire / Swamp, trapping, and every stat
stage on both sides are gone.** Whatever you built in the first half of the fight, you now rebuild.

Then:

> **ARCEUS HAS SEEN ENOUGH.**

This is where Phase 0's silent watching gets cashed in. Arceus takes a **permanent +40% to Defense
or Sp. Defense** — whichever half of your offence it saw more of — and opens the second half with
the Plate that counters you specifically:

| You've mostly been | It hardens | It opens with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical | **Defense** | **Fist** — Mist refuses your stat drops, and its Attack climbs |
| Special (or tied) | **Sp. Defense** | **Splash** — Swamp quarters your Speed, rain feeds it |

That stat gain is a **raw** change. Haze will not touch it and it is not bounded by the usual ±6.

You are told *that* it adapted. You are never told *to what*. Working out which half of your party
gave it away is the point.

From here Arceus invokes **a new Plate every single turn — and the telegraph stops.** No warning,
ever again.

---

### Phase 4 — The Original One (≤ 18%)

> *Arceus's body begins to radiate an impossible light.*
> **THE PLATES SHATTER.**
> **THE ORIGINAL ONE.**

No more Plates. Arceus stops defending — its damage reduction actually **drops** — takes +30% Sp.
Atk and +20% Speed, and rewrites **all four** of its moves with the power of a different type each:
**Judgment, Earth Power, Ice Beam, Shadow Ball.** There is nothing left to prepare for.

Decrees come back, now on a two-turn cadence.

It also **cannot be knocked out from here.** The finale is guaranteed to play.

---

### Judgment: Creation — the five-turn finale

Three turns into Phase 4:

> **ARCEUS PREPARES TO PASS JUDGMENT UPON THE WORLD.**

And here is where the whole fight is settled. If you pushed **Authority to 3 or less**:

> **THE WORLD AWAITS YOUR RESPONSE.**  →  `CHALLENGE` / `SUBMIT`

If you did not, the choice is never offered, and it tells you exactly why:

> *Arceus's authority is absolute. There is nothing to do but endure.*

**CHALLENGE** starts the ladder. Arceus charges for five turns, and **one damaging hit per charge
turn** knocks a rung off it. The rung is printed every turn, so you can watch your own progress:

```
"Eighteen Plates orbit the gathering light."
"Twelve Plates orbit the gathering light."
"Eight Plates orbit the gathering light."
"Four Plates orbit the gathering light."
"One Plate orbits the gathering light."
       THE FINAL PLATE SHATTERS.
```

**Five turns, five breaks.** There is no setup turn, no healing turn, no free switch. Miss one and
the ladder stalls where it is.

| Outcome | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Shattered** (all five) | Arceus reels — it loses 8% of its own HP, **loses its next turn entirely**, drops another 5 Authority, and its guard falls further still |
| **Landed** (anything else) | **JUDGMENT: CREATION.** 55% of your max HP, and Arceus remakes the battle — every stat stage, the weather and every screen on the field are wiped, and its Authority is restored to maximum |

---

### Phase 5 — The Final Moment

> *Arceus stands silently.*
> **The last blow is yours to strike.**

No Plates. No Decrees. No Judgment. Nothing but Arceus and whatever you have left.

---

### The catch window

Pokéballs are **blocked for the entire fight** until Arceus reaches the Final Moment at 10% HP or
less:

> *The light around Arceus fades to almost nothing.*
> *It's weakened — now is the moment to catch it!*

Only then can you throw. Its catch rate at that point is **20** — the lowest of any legendary in the
game. Bring Ultra Balls, bring status, and bring patience.
